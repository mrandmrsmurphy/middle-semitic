## Forms

An I-aleph, III-yod verb. The first radical lengthens the vowel (*â*) and eats a second aleph; the third radical contracts with the preceding *a* (*ay* → *ê*) and the first vowel wins in hiatus (*âbê* + *t* → *âbêt*). See [[Morphophonology]] and the model pages [[Lexicon/ܐܟܠ]] and [[Lexicon/ܒܢܝ]]. The citation form is the 3m past. Each cell gives the romanization and the plain spelling.

### Imperative

The imperative is the second-person non-past without its prefix. Every imperative is written with an exclamation mark. Where the stem starts with a consonant cluster, the first syllable carries a schwa (*ə*), and the stress falls on the next syllable.

### Passive

The passive changes the vowel after the first consonant to long *û*; in the non-past the root-initial aleph returns as a consonant after it. The participle and infinitive change the second vowel. The imperative has no passive.
