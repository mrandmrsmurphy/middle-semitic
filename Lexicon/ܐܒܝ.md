### Definitions

- **G** *âbê* ܐܒܝ: to will, to want, to be willing.
- **Gp** *ʾûbê* ܐܘܒܝ: passive of G: to be wanted.
- **D** *ʾîbbê* ܐܝܒܝ: to insist on, to will strongly.
- **Dp** *ʾûbbê* ܐܘܒܝ: passive of D: to be insisted on.
- **C** *šâbê* ܫܐܒܝ: to make willing, to persuade, to induce.
- **Cp** *šûʾbê* ܫܘܐܒܝ: passive of C: to be persuaded.
- **tD** *tiʾîbbê* ܬܐܝܒܝ: to hold oneself back, to refuse, to be reluctant.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܐܒܝ; Gp ܐܘܒܝ; D ܐܝܒܝ; Dp ܐܘܒܝ; C ܫܐܒܝ; Cp ܫܘܐܒܝ; tD ܬܐܝܒܝ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
