### Definitions

- **G** *ramê* ܪܡܝ: to throw, to cast, to hurl.
- **Gp** *rûmê* ܪܘܡܝ: passive of G: to be thrown.
- **D** *rîmmê* ܪܝܡܝ: to pelt, to hurl repeatedly.
- **Dp** *rûmmê* ܪܘܡܝ: passive of D: to be pelted.
- **tD** *tirîmmê* ܬܪܝܡܝ: to throw oneself, to hurl oneself, to rush.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܪܡܝ; Gp ܪܘܡܝ; D ܪܝܡܝ; Dp ܪܘܡܝ; tD ܬܪܝܡܝ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
