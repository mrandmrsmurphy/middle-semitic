### Definitions

- **G** *kapar* ܟܦܪ: to cover, to conceal.
- **Gp** *kûpar* ܟܘܦܪ: passive of G: to be covered.
- **D** *kîppar* ܟܝܦܪ: to atone, to expiate, to forgive.
- **Dp** *kûppar* ܟܘܦܪ: passive of D: to be atoned for, to be forgiven.
- **tD** *tikîppar* ܬܟܝܦܪ: to make atonement, to be atoned for.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܟܦܪ; Gp ܟܘܦܪ; D ܟܝܦܪ; Dp ܟܘܦܪ; tD ܬܟܝܦܪ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
