### Definitions

- **G** *barak* ܒܪܟ: to kneel.
- **D** *bîrrak* ܒܝܪܟ: to bless.
- **Dp** *bûrrak* ܒܘܪܟ: passive of D: to be blessed.
- **C** *šabrak* ܫܒܪܟ: to make kneel, to bring to the knees.
- **Cp** *šûbrak* ܫܘܒܪܟ: passive of C: to be brought to the knees.
- **tD** *tibîrrak* ܬܒܝܪܟ: to bless oneself, to receive a blessing.
- This verb has no Gp stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܒܪܟ; D ܒܝܪܟ; Dp ܒܘܪܟ; C ܫܒܪܟ; Cp ܫܘܒܪܟ; tD ܬܒܝܪܟ.
