## Forms

Masculine declension. A normal masculine noun: 'a hundred' is counted and modified like any other noun, and a noun counted by it is singular. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Middle Semitic/Morphophonology]].
