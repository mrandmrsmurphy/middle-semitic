### Definitions

- **G** *yanaq* ܝܢܩ: to suck, to suckle.
- **Gp** *yûnaq* ܝܘܢܩ: passive of G: to be suckled.
- **C** *šênaq* ܫܝܢܩ: to nurse, to give suck to.
- **Cp** *šûynaq* ܫܘܝܢܩ: passive of C: to be nursed.
- This verb has no D, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܝܢܩ; Gp ܝܘܢܩ; C ܫܝܢܩ; Cp ܫܘܝܢܩ.
