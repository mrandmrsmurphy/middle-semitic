Branch vote: NW (Hebrew and Aramaic) and Arabic share the ʾ-M-M noun “nation, people”; ruling by shared usage. In [[Tanakh#Leviticus 19:18]], *bênîn dal ʾummatâkâ* means “members of your people.” [Hebrew](https://www.pealim.com/he/dict/7503-uma/) · [Aramaic](https://cal.huc.edu/cal_entry_web.php?lemma=%29wmh+N) · [Arabic](https://corpus.quran.com/qurandictionary.jsp?q=Amm) |
