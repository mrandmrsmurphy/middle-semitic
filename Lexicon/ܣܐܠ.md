### Definitions

- **G** *saʾal* ܣܐܠ: to ask, to inquire, to borrow.
- **Gp** *sûʾal* ܣܘܐܠ: passive of G: to be asked.
- **D** *sîʾʾal* ܣܝܐܠ: to question closely, to interrogate.
- **Dp** *sûʾʾal* ܣܘܐܠ: passive of D: to be questioned.
- **C** *šasʾal* ܫܣܐܠ: to lend, to let (someone) borrow.
- **Cp** *šûsʾal* ܫܘܣܐܠ: passive of C: to be lent.
- **tD** *tisîʾʾal* ܬܣܝܐܠ: to ask one another, to wonder (assumes a plural subject).

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܣܐܠ; Gp ܣܘܐܠ; D ܣܝܐܠ; Dp ܣܘܐܠ; C ܫܣܐܠ; Cp ܫܘܣܐܠ; tD ܬܣܝܐܠ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
