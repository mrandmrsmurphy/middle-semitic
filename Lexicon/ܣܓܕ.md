| Notes | To bow down, to prostrate oneself, to worship: the one who bows takes *li-* (*lâ tasgad lahûm* "you shall not bow down to them", Exodus 20:5). Aramaic סגד *seged* "bow down in respect, worship" (Syriac ܣܓܕ), Hebrew סָגַד *sagád* "bow down, worship, honor" (rare, Isaiah and Daniel) and Arabic سَجَدَ *sajada* "prostrate oneself, bow down"; Wiktionary derives the Arabic from Aramaic, so it is a loan, though the root is the same. Consonants by the chart on [[Anthropology]]: PS *g* is MS ܓ (Arabic ج). Branch vote: S-G-D in Hebrew, Aramaic and Arabic; Hebrew's ordinary word שָׁחָה (Š-Ḥ-W, Hishtaphel) is Hebrew and Ugaritic only and is not used. Only the G stem exists. Hebrew, Aramaic and Arabic confirmed on Wiktionary; Ugaritic and the outgroups not checked |
