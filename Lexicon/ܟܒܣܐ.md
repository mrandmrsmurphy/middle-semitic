| Notes | The act of bringing under, and the state of being brought under: conquest, subjugation, submission. Syriac ܟܒܫܐ (*kəḇāšā*) is "pressure; assault, conquest, taking by storm" (Wiktionary, from the Syriac lexica); Modern Hebrew כִּבּוּשׁ is "conquest, occupation, suppressing" (Klein: "conquest, capture; pressing"), the verbal noun of כִּבֵּשׁ; Arabic كَبْس is "pressure, pressing; raid, attack". The vowel pattern is the Arabic one (*kabs*), as in [[Lexicon/ܐܡܪܐ\|ܐܡܪܐ]]. The verb is [[Lexicon/ܟܒܣ\|ܟܒܣ]]. Hebrew and Syriac readings are from Sefaria and Wiktionary; Ugaritic not checked. |

## Forms

Masculine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
