| Notes | Something that stands for something else by likeness: a simile, a proverb, a parable, a representation. Hebrew מָשָׁל is "proverb, parable, byword" (from מָשַׁל "be like"); Arabic مَثَل is "example, proverb, parable, likeness" (verb مَثَلَ "resemble, be like"; مِثَال "model", تِمْثَال "statue"); Syriac ܡܬܠܐ is "parable". The consonants follow the chart: PS *ṯ is Hebrew ש, Arabic ث, Aramaic ת, MS ܫ. The made object (doll, statue) is [[Lexicon/ܕܡܝ\|ܕܡܝ]], the shape or picture [[Lexicon/ܨܘܪܬܐ\|ܨܘܪܬܐ]]. Akkadian *mašālu* "be like" is recalled, unverified. Ugaritic not checked. |

## Forms

Masculine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
