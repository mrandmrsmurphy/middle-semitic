### Definitions

- **G** *qôm* ܩܘܡ: to arise, to stand.
- **Gp** *qûm* ܩܘܡ: passive of G: to be stood up.
- **D** *qîwwam* ܩܝܘܡ: to straighten, to set right.
- **Dp** *qûwwam* ܩܘܘܡ: passive of D: to be straightened.
- **C** *šaqwam* ܫܩܘܡ: to establish.
- **Cp** *šûqwam* ܫܘܩܘܡ: passive of C: to be established.
- **tD** *tiqîwwam* ܬܩܝܘܡ: to become straight.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܩܘܡ; Gp ܩܘܡ; D ܩܝܘܡ; Dp ܩܘܘܡ; C ܫܩܘܡ; Cp ܫܘܩܘܡ; tD ܬܩܝܘܡ. G and Gp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
