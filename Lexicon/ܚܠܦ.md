### Definitions

- **G** *ḥalap* ܚܠܦ: to pass, to pass by, to replace.
- **Gp** *ḥûlap* ܚܘܠܦ: passive of G: to be replaced.
- **D** *ḥîllap* ܚܝܠܦ: to leave behind, to appoint as successor.
- **Dp** *ḥûllap* ܚܘܠܦ: passive of D: to be left behind.
- **tD** *tiḥîllap* ܬܚܝܠܦ: to alternate, to change places, to be replaced in turn.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܠܦ; Gp ܚܘܠܦ; D ܚܝܠܦ; Dp ܚܘܠܦ; tD ܬܚܝܠܦ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
