### Definitions

- **G** *ḥaṭâ* ܚܛܐ: to sin, to commit a moral wrong.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, C, Cp, tD stems.

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܛܐ.
