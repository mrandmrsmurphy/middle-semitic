| Notes | Derived from PS *ʿṣr* by the consonant chart on [[Anthropology]]; every radical is regular (ʿ is ʿ, Arabic ص and Hebrew and Aramaic צ are ṣ, r is r). Arabic عَصَرَ means "press, squeeze, wring, extract juice from" (Egyptian and Palestinian the same); Jewish Aramaic עֲצַר "press, squeeze" (Targum Gen 40:11, Judges 6:38). Biblical Hebrew עָצַר is "restrain, retain, shut up", and "press, squeeze" appears only in Mishnaic Hebrew (Klein lists it as a second root); MS keeps the squeeze sense and leaves "restrain" to the Hebrew. Ge'ez ዐጸረ "press" and Akkadian *eṣēru* "retain, restrain" are cited by BDB (the Akkadian with "prob."). Hebrew לָחַץ (L-Ḥ-Ṣ) and Syriac ܩܡܛ (Q-M-Ṭ) are the regional words for the same action and are not taken. Arabic عَصْر "age, era, late afternoon" is a separate word in Wiktionary and is not linked here. |

### Definitions

- **G** *ʿaṣar* ܥܨܪ: to squeeze.
- **Gp** *ʿûṣar* ܥܘܨܪ: passive of G: to be squeezed.
- **D** *ʿîṣṣar* ܥܝܨܪ: to squeeze hard, to wring out.
- **tD** *tiʿîṣṣar* ܬܥܝܨܪ: to squeeze oneself, to be crowded.
- This verb has no Dp, C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܥܨܪ; Gp ܥܘܨܪ; D ܥܝܨܪ; tD ܬܥܝܨܪ.
