| Notes | A made likeness of a person or thing: an effigy, a doll, a statue. Arabic دُمْيَة is "statue, doll, effigy" (Wiktionary: perhaps from *dam* "blood", red ochre, or borrowed); Hebrew דְּמוּת is "likeness" (Gen 1:26, 5:1) from דָּמָה "be like", and Syriac ܕܡܘܬܐ "likeness, image, form, simile". MS takes the root and the Arabic sense, the made object, and leaves the abstract "likeness" to Hebrew and Aramaic; the stem is the III-yod noun *damy-*, like [[Lexicon/ܓܕܝ\|ܓܕܝ]] and [[Lexicon/ܣܡܝ\|ܣܡܝ]]. Feminine in Hebrew and Arabic, so crypto-feminine. Proto-Semitic and Ugaritic not checked. |

## Forms

Crypto-feminine declension of a noun from a III-yod root: the final *y* of the stem surfaces as *ê* in the singular; the plural and construct are the feminine ones, as in *samê*. There is no dual. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]].
