## Forms

An existence particle, not a verb: it does not inflect for person or number. The past is *hô* (*hô ôkâ*, 'there was an owl'); the non-past is the particle [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܝܫ|ܝܫ]] (*yiš*; *yiš êyalâ*, 'there is a stag'). Equality is a different construction, with the verb [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܟܘܢ|ܟܘܢ]]. See [[Middle Semitic/Morphosyntax]].
