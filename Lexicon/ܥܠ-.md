## Forms

One of the eight small prepositions that take the pronominal suffixes (see [[Morphosyntax]]). The inflected forms exist as words of their own:

| | form | gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1cs | [[Lexicon/ܥܠܢܝ\|ܥܠܢܝ]] *ʿalnî* | upon me |
| 2ms | [[Lexicon/ܥܠܟܐ\|ܥܠܟܐ]] *ʿalkâ* | upon you (m) |
| 2fs | [[Lexicon/ܥܠܟܝ\|ܥܠܟܝ]] *ʿalkî* | upon you (f) |
| 3ms | [[Lexicon/ܥܠܗܘ\|ܥܠܗܘ]] *ʿalhû* | upon him |
| 3fs | [[Lexicon/ܥܠܗܐ\|ܥܠܗܐ]] *ʿalhâ* | upon her |
| 1cp | [[Lexicon/ܥܠܢܘ\|ܥܠܢܘ]] *ʿalnû* | upon us |
| 2cp | [[Lexicon/ܥܠܟܘܡ\|ܥܠܟܘܡ]] *ʿalkûm* | upon y'all |
| 3cp | [[Lexicon/ܥܠܗܘܡ\|ܥܠܗܘܡ]] *ʿalhûm* | upon them |

The full word keeps its stress. Before a following word it has a clitic form, [[Lexicon/ܥ|ܥ]] *ʿa-*, with its own suffixed forms (*ʿanî*, *ʿakâ*, ...); in fast speech that clitic may be said *ʿə-*.
