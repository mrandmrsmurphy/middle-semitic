### Definitions

- **G** *âtê* ܐܬܝ: to come.
- **C** *šâtê* ܫܐܬܝ: to bring.
- **Cp** *šûʾtê* ܫܘܐܬܝ: passive of C: to be brought.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܐܬܝ; C ܫܐܬܝ; Cp ܫܘܐܬܝ.
