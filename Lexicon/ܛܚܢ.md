### Definitions

- **G** *ṭaḥan* ܛܚܢ: to grind, to mill.
- **Gp** *ṭûḥan* ܛܘܚܢ: passive of G: to be ground.
- **D** *ṭîḥân* ܛܝܚܐܢ: to grind finely, to pulverize.
- **Dp** *ṭûḥân* ܛܘܚܐܢ: passive of D: to be ground fine.
- **tD** *tiṭîḥân* ܬܛܝܚܐܢ: to be ground down, to wear away.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܛܚܢ; Gp ܛܘܚܢ; D ܛܝܚܐܢ; Dp ܛܘܚܐܢ; tD ܬܛܝܚܐܢ.
