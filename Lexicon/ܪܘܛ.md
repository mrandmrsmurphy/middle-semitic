| Notes | The Ugaritic spelling supports reconstruction with an emphatic interdental *ṱ*, which [[Anthropology]] maps to ܛ. The hollow stem has ô. The comparison with Syriac ܪܗܛ is plausible, with a different medial consonant. Branch vote: NW (Hebrew and Ugaritic) R-W-Ṭ “run,” alongside Aramaic R-H-Ṭ; Arabic G-R-Y and R-K-Ṣ; outgroup Geʿez *roṣa* supports the hollow root; ruling by breadth and continuity of sense. Akkadian *râṣu* means “come to help” and its connection is uncertain.
