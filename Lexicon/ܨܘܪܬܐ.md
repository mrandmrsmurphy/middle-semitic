| Notes | The plain word for an image as a shape: the form of a thing, a picture of it, the image in which something is made, and the image of God. Arabic صُورَة is "form, image, picture, photograph" in Standard Arabic and the spoken varieties alike, and is the word of the hadith "God created Adam in His image (*ṣūra*)"; Syriac ܨܘܪܬܐ is "image, picture, statue, form"; Hebrew צוּרָה is a rare word for "form, shape" (Ezekiel). Wiktionary treats the Arabic as probably a loan through Aramaic from Akkadian *iṣurtu* "drawing, plan", so no Proto-Semitic form is given. Branch vote: NW (Aramaic, Hebrew) Ṣ-W-R; Arabic Ṣ-W-R; Akkadian *iṣurtu* is the likely source; ruling by splitting senses: Ṣ-W-R takes "form, image, picture", [[Lexicon/ܨܠܡܐ\|ܨܠܡܐ]] keeps the cult image, [[Lexicon/ܕܡܝ\|ܕܡܝ]] the effigy and doll, [[Lexicon/ܡܫܠܐ\|ܡܫܠܐ]] the simile and representation. Ugaritic not checked. |

## Forms

True feminine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
