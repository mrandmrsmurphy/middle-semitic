### Definitions

- **G** *šahad* ܫܗܕ: to testify, to witness.
- **Gp** *šûhad* ܫܘܗܕ: passive of G: to be witnessed.
- **D** *šîhâd* ܫܝܗܐܕ: to attest, to certify.
- **Dp** *šûhâd* ܫܘܗܐܕ: passive of D: to be certified.
- **C** *šašhad* ܫܫܗܕ: to call as witness, to have (someone) testify.
- **Cp** *šûšhad* ܫܘܫܗܕ: passive of C: to be called as witness.
- **tD** *tišîhâd* ܬܫܝܗܐܕ: to give the testimony, to profess, to bear witness formally.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܫܗܕ; Gp ܫܘܗܕ; D ܫܝܗܐܕ; Dp ܫܘܗܐܕ; C ܫܫܗܕ; Cp ܫܘܫܗܕ; tD ܬܫܝܗܐܕ.
