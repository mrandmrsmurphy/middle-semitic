### Definitions

- **G** *wadaʿ* ܘܕܥ: to know.
- **Gp** *wûdaʿ* ܘܘܕܥ: passive of G: to be known.
- **C** *šôdaʿ* ܫܘܕܥ: to inform, to announce, to make known.
- **Cp** *šûwdaʿ* ܫܘܘܕܥ: passive of C: to be informed.
- **tD** *tiwîddaʿ* ܬܘܝܕܥ: to become acquainted, to introduce oneself, to become known.
- This verb has no D, Dp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܘܕܥ; Gp ܘܘܕܥ; C ܫܘܕܥ; Cp ܫܘܘܕܥ; tD ܬܘܝܕܥ.
