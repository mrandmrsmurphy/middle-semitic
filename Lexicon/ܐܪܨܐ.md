## Forms

Crypto-feminine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism, but it is the natural form on this word because of the many ancient texts. See [[Morphophonology]].

| | singular | plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| regular | ܐܰܪܨܰܐ *ʾarṣâ* ܐܪܨܐ | ܐܰܪܨܽܘܬ *ʾarṣût* ܐܪܨܘܬ |
| *construct* | ܐܰܪܨܰܬ *ʾarṣat* ܐܪܨܬ | ܐܰܪܨܽܘ *ʾarṣû* ܐܪܨܘ |

### With pronominal suffixes

In the sense 'country' the word takes the pronominal suffixes, attached to the construct state with nothing dropped.
