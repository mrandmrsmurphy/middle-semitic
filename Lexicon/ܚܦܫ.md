### Definitions

- **G** *ḥafaš* ܚܦܫ: to gather, to search out.
- **Gp** *ḥûfaš* ܚܘܦܫ: passive of G: to be gathered, to be searched out.
- **D** *ḥîppaš* ܚܝܦܫ: to look for, to search for, to seek.
- **Dp** *ḥûppaš* ܚܘܦܫ: passive of D: to be sought.
- **tD** *tiḥîppaš* ܬܚܝܦܫ: to disguise oneself.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܦܫ; Gp ܚܘܦܫ; D ܚܝܦܫ; Dp ܚܘܦܫ; tD ܬܚܝܦܫ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
