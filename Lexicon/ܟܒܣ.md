| Notes | To bring under one's foot: to subdue, subjugate, tread down (Gen 1:28). Hebrew כָּבַשׁ is "subdue, subjugate; tread down, level" (BDB; Qal, Niphal "be subdued"), Modern Hebrew כִּבֵּשׁ "conquer, occupy"; Syriac ܟܒܫ is the corresponding verb, Neo-Aramaic *kāḇēš* "invade, conquer, subjugate, tread down"; Arabic كَبَسَ is "press, squeeze; attack, raid" (Wiktionary), the same root with the pressing sense. Consonants by the chart on [[Anthropology]]: PS *š* is Hebrew שׁ, Arabic س, Aramaic ܫ, MS ܣ, so the root is ܟ-ܒ-ܣ. Branch vote: NW (Hebrew, Aramaic) K-B-Š "subdue"; Arabic K-B-S "press, raid" and, for "subdue", other roots (قَهَرَ, غَلَبَ, أَخْضَعَ); ruling by the shared root, with the Arabic pressing sense kept as the D stem (كَبَّسَ "press hard"). Akkadian *kabāsu* "tread" is recalled, unverified; Ugaritic not checked. The Hebrew nouns כֶּבֶשׂ "sheep" and כִּבְשָׁן "furnace" are separate or doubtful. The noun is [[Lexicon/ܟܒܣܐ\|ܟܒܣܐ]]. |

### Definitions

- **G** *kabas* ܟܒܣ: to subdue, to subjugate, to tread down.
- **Gp** *kûbas* ܟܘܒܣ: passive of G: to be subdued.
- **D** *kîbbas* ܟܝܒܣ: to conquer, to occupy, to press hard.
- **Dp** *kûbbas* ܟܘܒܣ: passive of D: to be conquered.
- This verb has no C, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܟܒܣ; Gp ܟܘܒܣ; D ܟܝܒܣ; Dp ܟܘܒܣ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
