## Forms

A III-waw stative verb. The third radical is always written. It contracts with the preceding *a* (*ay* → *ê*, *aw* → *ô*, *aʾ* → *â*: *banê*, *darô*, *qarâ*); if a vowel then follows, the first vowel wins (*banay* + *at* → *banêt*). Only where no *a* precedes it (the 3pl past, *banyû*) is it pronounced as a consonant. See [[Morphophonology]] and the model page [[Lexicon/ܡܠܟ]]. The citation form is the 3m past. Each cell gives the romanization and the plain spelling.

### Imperative

The imperative is the second-person non-past without its prefix. Every imperative is written with an exclamation mark. Where the stem starts with a consonant cluster, the first syllable carries a schwa (*ə*), and the stress falls on the next syllable.

| --- | --- | --- |

| f | *ḥəlû!* ܚܠܘܝ! | *ḥəlûnâ!* ܚܠܘܢܐ! |
