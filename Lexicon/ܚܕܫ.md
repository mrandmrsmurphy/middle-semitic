### Definitions

- **G** *ḥadaš* ܚܕܫ: to become new.
- **D** *ḥîddaš* ܚܝܕܫ: to renew, to innovate, to restore.
- **Dp** *ḥûddaš* ܚܘܕܫ: passive of D: to be renewed.
- **tD** *tiḥîddaš* ܬܚܝܕܫ: to renew oneself, to be renewed.
- This verb has no Gp, C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܕܫ; D ܚܝܕܫ; Dp ܚܘܕܫ; tD ܬܚܝܕܫ.
