### Definitions

- **G** *qanîʾ* ܩܢܝܐ: to be jealous, to be zealous for exclusive loyalty.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, C, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܩܢܝܐ.
