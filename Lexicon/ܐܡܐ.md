## Forms

Crypto-feminine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.

| | singular | plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| regular | *immâ* ܐܡܐ | *immût* ܐܡܘܬ |
| *construct* | *immat* ܐܡܬ | *immû* ܐܡܘ |
