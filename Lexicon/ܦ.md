## Forms

One of the eight small prepositions that take the pronominal suffixes (see [[Morphosyntax]]). The inflected forms exist as words of their own:

| 2ms | [[Lexicon/ܦܟܐ\|ܦܟܐ]] *pikâ* | in you (m) |

### Fast speech

A clitic: it attaches to the following word and takes no stress. In fast speech it is acceptable to say it with a schwa for its vowel, as though *pi-* were *pə-*: *pəMadrid* = in Madrid, within Madrid (for *piMadrid*). The schwa is marginal elsewhere; see [[Phonology]].
