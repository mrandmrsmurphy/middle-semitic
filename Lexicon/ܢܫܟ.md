### Definitions

- **G** *našak* ܢܫܟ: to bite.
- **Gp** *nûšak* ܢܘܫܟ: passive of G: to be bitten.
- This verb has no D, Dp, C, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܢܫܟ; Gp ܢܘܫܟ.
