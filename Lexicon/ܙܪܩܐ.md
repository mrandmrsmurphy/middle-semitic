| Notes | The colour comes from Z-R-Q, the Arabic root of أَزْرَق "blue" (Wiktionary compares the semantic development to Greek γλαυκός "gleaming"). The Hebrew and Aramaic cognates are the same root with a different sense: Hebrew זָרַק and Aramaic זְרַק / Syriac ܙܪܰܩ "throw, toss, scatter" (BDB, which also compares Arabic زَرَقَ "cast at, pierce"). Hebrew has no colour word from this root; its words for blue are כָּחֹל and תְּכֵלֶת. |

## Forms

Adjective: it agrees with its noun in gender and number, and has the four forms below (masculine *-â*, feminine *-tâ*, plural *-în* and *-ût*). The construct forms are frozen and an archaism. A crypto-feminine noun takes the feminine forms. See [[Morphophonology]].
