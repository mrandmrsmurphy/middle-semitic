| Notes | The feminine diminutive of [[Lexicon/ܒܚܪܐ\|ܒܚܪܐ]] *baḥrâ* "sea": a lake is a little sea. Arabic بُحَيْرَة is likewise the diminutive of بَحْر, and the word is feminine in the sources, so the feminine diminutive *-ôta* is used, not the masculine *-ôna* (see [[Morphophonology#Nouns]]). Syriac makes its lake the same way on the other sea-word: ܝܡܬܐ is the feminine of ܝܡܐ "sea" (the Aramaic row holds the related ܒܚܪܐ "sea"). The Sumerian-borrowed *ʾagamâ* family covers the marshy shore: [[Lexicon/ܐܓܡܐ\|ܐܓܡܐ]]. |

## Forms

True feminine declension (a diminutive declines by its own gender). There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]].
