### Definitions

- **G** *saḥan* ܣܚܢ: to heat, to warm up.
- **Gp** *sûḥan* ܣܘܚܢ: passive of G: to be heated.
- **D** *sîḥân* ܣܝܚܐܢ: to heat thoroughly, to bring up to heat.
- **Dp** *sûḥân* ܣܘܚܐܢ: passive of D: to be heated thoroughly.
- **tD** *tisîḥân* ܬܣܝܚܐܢ: to warm oneself, to heat up gradually.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܣܚܢ; Gp ܣܘܚܢ; D ܣܝܚܐܢ; Dp ܣܘܚܐܢ; tD ܬܣܝܚܐܢ.
