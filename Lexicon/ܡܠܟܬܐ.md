## Forms

True feminine declension. The construct state is frozen and an archaism, but it is the natural form on these words because of the many ancient texts (compare Hebrew *avinu malkeinu*, 'our father, our king'). The dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one, so 'two queens' is the plural. See [[Morphophonology]].

| | singular | plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| regular | ܡܰܠܟܰܬܱܐ *malkatâ* ܡܠܟܬܐ | ܡܰܠܟܽܘܬ *malkût* ܡܠܟܘܬ |
| *construct* | ܡܰܠܟܰܬ *malkat* ܡܠܟܬ | ܡܰܠܟܽܘ *malkû* ܡܠܟܘ |

### With pronominal suffixes

The pronominal suffixes attach to the construct state, with nothing dropped: singular *malkat* + suffix, plural *malkû* + suffix.
