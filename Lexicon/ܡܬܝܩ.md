### Definitions

- **G** *matîq* ܡܬܝܩ: to be sweet, to be delicious.
- **D** *mîttaq* ܡܝܬܩ: to sweeten.
- **Dp** *mûttaq* ܡܘܬܩ: passive of D: to be sweetened.
- **C** *šamtaq* ܫܡܬܩ: to make delicious.
- **Cp** *šûmtaq* ܫܘܡܬܩ: passive of C: to be made delicious.
- **tD** *timîttaq* ܬܡܝܬܩ: to become sweet, to sweeten up.
- This verb has no Gp stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܡܬܝܩ; D ܡܝܬܩ; Dp ܡܘܬܩ; C ܫܡܬܩ; Cp ܫܘܡܬܩ; tD ܬܡܝܬܩ.
