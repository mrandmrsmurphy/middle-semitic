### Definitions

- **G** *ṣaḥak* ܨܚܟ: to laugh.
- **D** *ṣîḥâk* ܨܝܚܐܟ: to jest, to mock.
- **Dp** *ṣûḥâk* ܨܘܚܐܟ: passive of D: to be mocked.
- **C** *šaṣḥak* ܫܨܚܟ: to make laugh, to amuse.
- **Cp** *šûṣḥak* ܫܘܨܚܟ: passive of C: to be amused.
- This verb has no Gp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܨܚܟ; D ܨܝܚܐܟ; Dp ܨܘܚܐܟ; C ܫܨܚܟ; Cp ܫܘܨܚܟ.
