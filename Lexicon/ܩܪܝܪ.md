### Definitions

- **G** *qarîr* ܩܪܝܪ: to be cold.
- **D** *qîrrar* ܩܝܪܪ: to cool, to chill.
- **Dp** *qûrrar* ܩܘܪܪ: passive of D: to be cooled, to be chilled.
- **tD** *tiqîrrar* ܬܩܝܪܪ: to cool down, to catch a chill.
- This verb has no Gp, C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܩܪܝܪ; D ܩܝܪܪ; Dp ܩܘܪܪ; tD ܬܩܝܪܪ.
