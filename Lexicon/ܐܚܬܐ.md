## Forms

True feminine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Middle Semitic/Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.

| | singular | plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| regular | *aḥtâ* ܐܚܬܐ | *aḥût* ܐܚܘܬ |
| *construct* | *aḥat* ܐܚܬ | *aḥû* ܐܚܘ |

### With pronominal suffixes

The pronominal suffixes attach to the construct state, with nothing dropped.

| | singular noun | plural noun |
| --- | --- | --- |
