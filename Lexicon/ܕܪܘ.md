### Definitions

- **G** *darô* ܕܪܘ: to winnow, to scatter.
- **Gp** *dûrô* ܕܘܪܘ: passive of G: to be winnowed, to be scattered.
- **D** *dîrrô* ܕܝܪܘ: to scatter widely, to disperse.
- **Dp** *dûrrô* ܕܘܪܘ: passive of D: to be dispersed.
- **tD** *tidîrrô* ܬܕܝܪܘ: to scatter, to disperse, to be carried away.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܕܪܘ; Gp ܕܘܪܘ; D ܕܝܪܘ; Dp ܕܘܪܘ; tD ܬܕܝܪܘ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
