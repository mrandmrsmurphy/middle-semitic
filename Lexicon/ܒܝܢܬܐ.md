## Forms

True feminine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.

| | singular | plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| regular | *bêntâ* ܒܝܢܬܐ | *bênût* ܒܝܢܘܬ |
| *construct* | *bênat* ܒܝܢܬ | *bênû* ܒܝܢܘ |

### With pronominal suffixes

The pronominal suffixes attach to the construct state, with nothing dropped.
