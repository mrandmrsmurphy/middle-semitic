### Definitions

- **G** *qarab* ܩܪܒ: to come near, to approach.
- **C** *šaqrab* ܫܩܪܒ: to offer, to present, to bring near.
- **Cp** *šûqrab* ܫܘܩܪܒ: passive of C: to be offered.
- **tD** *tiqîrrab* ܬܩܝܪܒ: to draw near, to approach gradually.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܩܪܒ; C ܫܩܪܒ; Cp ܫܘܩܪܒ; tD ܬܩܝܪܒ.
