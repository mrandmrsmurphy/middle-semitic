### Definitions

- **G** *nadô* ܢܕܘ: to summon, to call, to cast out.
- **Gp** *nûdô* ܢܘܕܘ: passive of G: to be summoned, to be cast out.
- **D** *nîddô* ܢܝܕܘ: to proclaim, to announce, to call out.
- **Dp** *nûddô* ܢܘܕܘ: passive of D: to be proclaimed.
- **tD** *tinîddô* ܬܢܝܕܘ: to call to one another, to assemble (assumes a plural subject).
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܢܕܘ; Gp ܢܘܕܘ; D ܢܝܕܘ; Dp ܢܘܕܘ; tD ܬܢܝܕܘ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
