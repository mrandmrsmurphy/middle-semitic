| Notes | The place that is near: nearness, the vicinity. Arabic قُرْب *qurb* is the verbal noun of *qaruba* "be near" ("nearness, presence"); Hebrew קֶרֶב *qerev* is "inward part, midst", the same noun with a narrower sense. The preposition is [[Lexicon/ܩܪܒ 2|ܩܪܒ]] *qurab* "near". The form follows the regular masculine template (*qurbâ*, like [[Lexicon/ܬܚܬܐ|ܬܚܬܐ]]); the coda *r* is written and pronounced. |

## Forms

Masculine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
