### Definitions

- **G** *lôš* ܠܘܫ: to knead, to twist.
- **Gp** *lûš* ܠܘܫ: passive of G: to be kneaded, to be twisted.
- **D** *lîwwaš* ܠܝܘܫ: to knead thoroughly, to wind tightly.
- **Dp** *lûwwaš* ܠܘܘܫ: passive of D: to be kneaded thoroughly.
- **tD** *tilîwwaš* ܬܠܝܘܫ: to become entwined, to blend together.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܠܘܫ; Gp ܠܘܫ; D ܠܝܘܫ; Dp ܠܘܘܫ; tD ܬܠܝܘܫ. G and Gp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
