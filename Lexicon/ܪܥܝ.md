### Definitions

- **G** *raʿê* ܪܥܝ: to herd, to shepherd, to pasture.
- **Gp** *rûʿê* ܪܘܥܝ: passive of G: to be herded.
- **D** *rîʿê* ܪܝܥܐܝ: to tend closely, to shepherd with care.
- **Dp** *rûʿê* ܪܘܥܐܝ: passive of D: to be tended closely.
- **C** *šarʿê* ܫܪܥܝ: to put out to pasture, to let graze.
- **Cp** *šûrʿê* ܫܘܪܥܝ: passive of C: to be put out to pasture.
- **tD** *tirîʿê* ܬܪܝܥܐܝ: to come under someone's care, to be looked after.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܪܥܝ; Gp ܪܘܥܝ; D ܪܝܥܐܝ; Dp ܪܘܥܐܝ; C ܫܪܥܝ; Cp ܫܘܪܥܝ; tD ܬܪܝܥܐܝ.
