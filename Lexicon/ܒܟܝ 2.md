### Definitions

- **G** *bakê* ܒܟܝ: to cry, to weep.
- **C** *šabkê* ܫܒܟܝ: to make someone cry.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܒܟܝ; C ܫܒܟܝ.
