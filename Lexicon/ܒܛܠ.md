### Definitions

- **G** *baṭal* ܒܛܠ: to become void, to cease.
- **D** *bîṭṭal* ܒܝܛܠ: to nullify, to cancel.
- **Dp** *bûṭṭal* ܒܘܛܠ: passive of D: to be nullified.
- **C** *šabṭal* ܫܒܛܠ: to annul, to render void.
- **Cp** *šûbṭal* ܫܘܒܛܠ: passive of C: to be annulled.
- **tD** *tibîṭṭal* ܬܒܝܛܠ: to be annulled, to lapse.
- This verb has no Gp stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܒܛܠ; D ܒܝܛܠ; Dp ܒܘܛܠ; C ܫܒܛܠ; Cp ܫܘܒܛܠ; tD ܬܒܝܛܠ.
