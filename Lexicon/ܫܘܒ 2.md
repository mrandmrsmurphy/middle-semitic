| Notes | The adverb of the root of [[Lexicon/ܫܘܒ\|ܫܘܒ]] "to return"; Hebrew does the same with one word — שׁוּב is both the verb "to return" and the adverb "again, once more" (Klein; the adverb is Mishnaic, Jastrow Nidd. V,7, and modern). Aramaic ܬܘܒ "again" is recalled from the Peshitta, unverified. Arabic has no adverb from this root: its "again" is مَرَّةً أُخْرَى "another time", عَادَ + verb (root ʿ-W-D, unrelated), or dialectal تاني (from "second"); the root survives in Arabic as the verbs ثَابَ "to return" (rare) and تَابَ "to repent". Placement is final, as in Hebrew (זוֹרַחַת שׁוּב) and the dialects (عم تشرق تاني). Homograph of the verb [[Lexicon/ܫܘܒ\|ܫܘܒ]]. |
