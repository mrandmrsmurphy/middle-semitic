### Definitions

- **G** *banê* ܒܢܝ: to build.
- **Gp** *bûnê* ܒܘܢܝ: passive of G: to be built.
- **C** *šabnê* ܫܒܢܝ: to have (something) built, to commission.
- **Cp** *šûbnê* ܫܘܒܢܝ: passive of C: to be commissioned.
- **tD** *tibînnê* ܬܒܝܢܝ: to be built up, to take shape.
- This verb has no D, Dp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܒܢܝ; Gp ܒܘܢܝ; C ܫܒܢܝ; Cp ܫܘܒܢܝ; tD ܬܒܝܢܝ.
