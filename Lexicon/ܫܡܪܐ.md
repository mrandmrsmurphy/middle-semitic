| Notes | PS *ṯamar-* "fruit, crop; produce, gain" (Wiktionary): Arabic ثَمَر "fruit, crops, produce; result, profit" (collective; singulative ثَمَرَة), Sabaean *ṯmr* "fruits", Ge'ez ሰምር *sämr*, and Aramaic *tamrā* with the regular t for ṯ. The date is the same word in the northwest and in Arabic's second form: Aramaic תַּמְרָא "date", Hebrew תָּמָר "date, date palm" (BDB; an early loan from Aramaic, since native Hebrew would have š), Ugaritic *tmr* "dates" (UgaritLab), Arabic تَمْر "dates", Sabaean *tmr*. Consonants by the chart on [[Anthropology]]: PS *ṯ* is MS ܫ, so the root is ܫ-ܡ-ܪ (not ܣ-ܡ-ܪ, the root of the colour [[Lexicon/ܣܡܪܐ\|ܣܡܪܐ]] "brown"). Branch vote: NW (Hebrew, Aramaic, Ugaritic) P-R-Y "fruit" (Hebrew פְּרִי, Ugaritic *pry*) but Ṯ-M-R as the date; Arabic Ṯ-M-R; outgroups (Sabaean, Ge'ez) Ṯ-M-R; ruling by outgroups, with the date sense carried by the same root. Its verb is [[Lexicon/ܫܡܪ\|ܫܡܪ]]. Akkadian not checked. |

## Forms

Masculine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
