## Forms

Adjective from a III-yod root: it agrees with its noun in gender and number. The final *y* of the stem contracts with the endings (*ay* → *ê*, and where a vowel follows, the first vowel wins, as in the III-weak verbs): masculine *ʿabê*, *ʿabîn*; feminine *ʿabêtâ*, *ʿabêt*. The construct forms are frozen and an archaism, here built on the *samat, samû* model of [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܣܡܝ\|ܣܡܝ]]. A crypto-feminine noun takes the feminine forms. See [[Middle Semitic/Morphophonology]].
