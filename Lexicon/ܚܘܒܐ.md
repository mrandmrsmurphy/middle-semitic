| Notes | A debt owed in money or a moral debt that can be called to account or forgiven. Syriac ܚܘܒܐ covers both senses and renders Hebrew עָוֹן in Exodus 20:5. Qur’an 4:2 uses حُوب for a grave offense, possibly an Aramaic loan. The MS noun contracts *aw* to *ô*. Branch vote: NW (Aramaic) Ḥ-W-B; Arabic Ḥ-W-B in the moral sense, possibly borrowed; no independent outgroup established; ruling by attested Aramaic usage and Arabic moral usage. |


## Forms
