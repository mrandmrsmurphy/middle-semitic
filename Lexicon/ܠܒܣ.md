### Definitions

- **G** *lavas* ܠܒܣ: to wear, to put on.
- **Gp** *lûvas* ܠܘܒܣ: passive of G: to be worn.
- **D** *lîbbas* ܠܝܒܣ: to coat, to cover.
- **Dp** *lûbbas* ܠܘܒܣ: passive of D: to be coated, to be covered.
- **C** *šalbas* ܫܠܒܣ: to clothe, to dress (someone).
- **Cp** *šûlbas* ܫܘܠܒܣ: passive of C: to be clothed.
- **tD** *tilîbbas* ܬܠܝܒܣ: to get dressed, to dress oneself.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܠܒܣ; Gp ܠܘܒܣ; D ܠܝܒܣ; Dp ܠܘܒܣ; C ܫܠܒܣ; Cp ܫܘܠܒܣ; tD ܬܠܝܒܣ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
