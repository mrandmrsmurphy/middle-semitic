### Definitions

- **G** *zamar* ܙܡܪ: to sing, to make music.
- **Gp** *zûmar* ܙܘܡܪ: passive of G: to be sung, to be played.
- **D** *zîmmar* ܙܝܡܪ: to play (a pipe), to sing praise.
- **Dp** *zûmmar* ܙܘܡܪ: passive of D: to be played, to be sung in praise.
- **C** *šazmar* ܫܙܡܪ: to lead in song, to have (someone) sing.
- **Cp** *šûzmar* ܫܘܙܡܪ: passive of C: to be led in song.
- This verb has no tD stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܙܡܪ; Gp ܙܘܡܪ; D ܙܝܡܪ; Dp ܙܘܡܪ; C ܫܙܡܪ; Cp ܫܘܙܡܪ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
