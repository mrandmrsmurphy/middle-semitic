| Notes | The name of Egypt is M-Ṣ-R in every branch: Hebrew מִצְרַיִם *Miṣráyim*, Syriac ܡܨܪܝܢ, Arabic مِصْر *Miṣr* (also "Cairo" in the Egyptian dialect), Ugaritic *mṣrm*, Akkadian *Miṣri* / *Muṣur* (Wiktionary). The Hebrew and Ugaritic ending is the dual, perhaps for the two realms, Upper and Lower Egypt; MS has the bare singular, the Arabic form, because the dual is frozen and reserved for body parts. Hebrew מֵצֵר "border, limit" has been compared; Arabic also has a separate verb مَصَّرَ "found a city". Consonants by the chart on [[Anthropology]]: *ṣ* is MS ܨ. No branch vote was needed. Names do not inflect: no plural, construct or suffixed forms. |
