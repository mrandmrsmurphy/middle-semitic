| Notes | The upper leg between the hip and knee. Proto-West-Semitic *paḫiḏ-* gives ܦ-ܚ-ܙ by [[Anthropology]]; the shortened stem parallels Arabic فَخْذ. Branch vote: NW Hebrew Y-R-K, Syriac P-Ḥ-D “thigh, haunch”; Arabic F-Ḫ-Ḏ “thigh”; Sabaic and Modern South Arabian also attest this family; ruling by the shared root and the precise Arabic sense. The comparison with Job 40:17 is disputed. Compare [[Lexicon/ܘܪܟܐ\|ܘܪܟܐ]] “hip.” [Comparative evidence](https://datsemshift.ru/shift779) · [Job 40:17](https://openscholar.huji.ac.il/sites/default/files/he_bible_project/files/wolters_a.pdf). |
