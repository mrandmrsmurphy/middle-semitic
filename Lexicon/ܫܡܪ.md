The Hebrew תִּמֵּר "rise straight like a palm" (Jastrow) is a verb from the date-palm noun, a different sense. Consonants by the chart on [[Anthropology]]: PS *ṯ* is MS ܫ. ### Definitions

- **G** *šamar* ܫܡܪ: to be fruitful, to bear fruit.
- **C** *šašmar* ܫܫܡܪ: to make fruitful, to cause to bear fruit.
- **Cp** *šûšmar* ܫܘܫܡܪ: passive of C: to be made fruitful.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܫܡܪ; C ܫܫܡܪ; Cp ܫܘܫܡܪ.
