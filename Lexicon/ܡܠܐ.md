### Definitions

- **G** *malâ* ܡܠܐ: to fill.
- **Gp** *mûlâ* ܡܘܠܐ: passive of G: to be filled.
- **tD** *timîllâ* ܬܡܝܠܐ: to fill up, to become full.
- This verb has no D, Dp, C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܡܠܐ; Gp ܡܘܠܐ; tD ܬܡܝܠܐ.
