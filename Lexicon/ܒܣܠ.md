### Definitions

- **G** *basal* ܒܣܠ: to cook.
- **Gp** *bûsal* ܒܘܣܠ: passive of G: to be cooked.
- **C** *šabsal* ܫܒܣܠ: to have (someone) cook, to let cook.
- **Cp** *šûbsal* ܫܘܒܣܠ: passive of C: to be had cooked.
- **tD** *tibîssal* ܬܒܝܣܠ: to be cooked through, to ripen.
- This verb has no D, Dp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܒܣܠ; Gp ܒܘܣܠ; C ܫܒܣܠ; Cp ܫܘܒܣܠ; tD ܬܒܝܣܠ.
