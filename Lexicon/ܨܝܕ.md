### Definitions

- **G** *ṣîd* ܨܝܕ: to hunt.
- **Gp** *ṣûd* ܨܘܕ: passive of G: to be hunted.
- **D** *ṣîyyad* ܨܝܝܕ: to snare, to hunt at random, to dupe.
- **Dp** *ṣûyyad* ܨܘܝܕ: passive of D: to be snared, to be duped.
- **tD** *tiṣîyyad* ܬܨܝܝܕ: to be caught, to hunt for oneself.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܨܝܕ; Gp ܨܘܕ; D ܨܝܝܕ; Dp ܨܘܝܕ; tD ܬܨܝܝܕ.
