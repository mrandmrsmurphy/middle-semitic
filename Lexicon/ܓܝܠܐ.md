| Notes | A group of people born around the same time, such as the Baby Boomers. Arabic *jīl* has the cohort sense, while Hebrew *gîl* means “age.” [Klein](https://www.sefaria.org/Klein_Dictionary%2C_%D7%92%D7%99%D7%9C) also compares Samaritan גיל. Branch vote: NW (Hebrew, Samaritan) G-Y-L in the age sense; Arabic G-Y-L for a cohort; no outgroup counted; ruling by the attested Arabic cohort sense. For a generation in a family line, see [[Lexicon/ܕܘܪܐ]]. |

## Forms

Masculine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
