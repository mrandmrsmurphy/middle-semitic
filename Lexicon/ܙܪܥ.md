### Definitions

- **G** *zaraʿ* ܙܪܥ: to sow, to plant.
- **Gp** *zûraʿ* ܙܘܪܥ: passive of G: to be sown.
- **D** *zîrraʿ* ܙܝܪܥ: to sow widely, to scatter, to disseminate.
- **Dp** *zûrraʿ* ܙܘܪܥ: passive of D: to be scattered, to be disseminated.
- **C** *šazraʿ* ܫܙܪܥ: to make fertile, to have (someone) sow.
- **Cp** *šûzraʿ* ܫܘܙܪܥ: passive of C: to be made fertile.
- This verb has no tD stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܙܪܥ; Gp ܙܘܪܥ; D ܙܝܪܥ; Dp ܙܘܪܥ; C ܫܙܪܥ; Cp ܫܘܙܪܥ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
