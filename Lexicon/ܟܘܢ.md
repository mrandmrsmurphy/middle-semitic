### Definitions

- **G** *kôn* ܟܘܢ: to be, to be equal.
- **D** *kîwwan* ܟܝܘܢ: to form, to constitute, to establish.
- **Dp** *kûwwan* ܟܘܘܢ: passive of D: to be formed, to be constituted.
- **C** *šakwan* ܫܟܘܢ: to prepare, to set up.
- **Cp** *šûkwan* ܫܘܟܘܢ: passive of C: to be prepared.
- **tD** *tikîwwan* ܬܟܝܘܢ: to be formed, to take shape, to consist of.
- This verb has no Gp stem.
