| Notes          | The only relative pronoun, and it never inflects. From Maltese *li*, Aramaic ܕ, Levantine/Egyptian اللي and MSA ٱلَّذِي. See [[Morphophonology]]. Ugaritic *ḏ* is unverified: it is recalled as a relative and demonstrative pronoun (feminine *ḏt*), the same element as Aramaic ܕ; Wiktionary lists no Ugaritic descendant. |
| Example | *Please go meet Rachel, who is arriving now.* The pronoun stands for the noun it follows (Rachel) inside its own clause. Contrast [[Lexicon/ܐܢ\|ܐܢ]], the complementizer, which stands for no noun. |
