| Notes | A generation in a line of descent. The regular *aw* → *ô* contraction gives *dôrâ*. The Peshitta has plural ܕܪ̈ܝܢ in Exodus 20:5; Hebrew leaves the noun implicit there. Arabic *dawr* means “turn, phase, cycle,” and Akkadian *dūru* means “perpetuity.” Branch vote: NW (Hebrew, Aramaic, Ugaritic) D-W-R; Arabic G-Y-L for “generation,” D-W-R for “cycle”; Akkadian D-W-R supports the time sense; ruling by the attested family sense. For a group born around the same time, see [[Lexicon/ܓܝܠܐ]]. |
