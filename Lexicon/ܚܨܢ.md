### Definitions

- **G** *ḥaṣan* ܚܨܢ: to embrace, to hug.
- **Gp** *ḥûṣan* ܚܘܨܢ: passive of G: to be embraced.
- **D** *ḥîṣṣan* ܚܝܨܢ: to foster, to cherish, to bring up (a child).
- **Dp** *ḥûṣṣan* ܚܘܨܢ: passive of D: to be fostered.
- **tD** *tiḥîṣṣan* ܬܚܝܨܢ: to embrace one another (assumes a plural subject).
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܨܢ; Gp ܚܘܨܢ; D ܚܝܨܢ; Dp ܚܘܨܢ; tD ܬܚܝܨܢ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
