### Definitions

- **G** *ḥasab* ܚܣܒ: to think, to reckon, to count.
- **Gp** *ḥûsab* ܚܘܣܒ: passive of G: to be reckoned, to be considered.
- **D** *ḥîssab* ܚܝܣܒ: to calculate, to compute.
- **Dp** *ḥûssab* ܚܘܣܒ: passive of D: to be computed.
- **C** *šaḥsab* ܫܚܣܒ: to hold important, to value.
- **Cp** *šûḥsab* ܫܘܚܣܒ: passive of C: to be held important.
- **tD** *tiḥîssab* ܬܚܝܣܒ: to take into account, to be considerate.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܣܒ; Gp ܚܘܣܒ; D ܚܝܣܒ; Dp ܚܘܣܒ; C ܫܚܣܒ; Cp ܫܘܚܣܒ; tD ܬܚܝܣܒ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
