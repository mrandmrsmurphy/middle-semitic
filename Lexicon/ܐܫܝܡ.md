### Definitions

- **G** *âšîm* ܐܫܝܡ: to be guilty, to bear culpability.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, C, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܐܫܝܡ.
