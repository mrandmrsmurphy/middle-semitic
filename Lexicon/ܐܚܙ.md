### Definitions

- **G** *âḥaz* ܐܚܙ: to take, to seize.
- **Gp** *ʾûḥaz* ܐܘܚܙ: passive of G: to be taken, to be seized.
- **D** *ʾîḥâz* ܐܝܚܐܙ: to grasp firmly, to hold fast.
- **Dp** *ʾûḥâz* ܐܘܚܐܙ: passive of D: to be held fast.
- **tD** *tiʾîḥâz* ܬܐܝܚܐܙ: to hold on to, to cling.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܐܚܙ; Gp ܐܘܚܙ; D ܐܝܚܐܙ; Dp ܐܘܚܐܙ; tD ܬܐܝܚܐܙ.
