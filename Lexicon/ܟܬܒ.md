### Definitions

- **G** *katab* ܟܬܒ: to write.
- **Gp** *kûtab* ܟܘܬܒ: passive of G: to be written.
- **D** *kîttab* ܟܝܬܒ: to inscribe.
- **Dp** *kûttab* ܟܘܬܒ: passive of D: to be inscribed.
- **C** *šaktab* ܫܟܬܒ: to dictate.
- **Cp** *šûktab* ܫܘܟܬܒ: passive of C: to be dictated.
- **tD** *tikîttab* ܬܟܝܬܒ: to correspond: they corresponded, they exchanged letters (assumes a plural subject).

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܟܬܒ; Gp ܟܘܬܒ; D ܟܝܬܒ; Dp ܟܘܬܒ; C ܫܟܬܒ; Cp ܫܘܟܬܒ; tD ܬܟܝܬܒ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
