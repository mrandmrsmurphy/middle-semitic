| Notes | The sense of Biblical Hebrew *wayəhî* "and it came to pass" (from הָיָה); Arabic وَقَعَ is "fall, occur, happen, befall" in MSA and in the Egyptian and Palestinian dialects (Maltese *waqaʿ*). Derived from PS *wqʿ* by the consonant chart on [[Anthropology]]: *w*, *q*, *ʿ* are MS w, q, ʿ. The Hebrew cognate יָקַע means only "be dislocated; be alienated" (Hiphil הוֹקִיעַ "hang, impale"), so the senses are far apart. "To fall (down)" is [[Lexicon/ܢܦܠ\|ܢܦܠ]]; this verb takes the "happen" sense, as Hebrew נָפַל does for "fall out, turn out". Aramaic not checked. Ugaritic not checked. |

### Definitions

- **G** *waqaʿ* ܘܩܥ: to happen, to befall.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, C, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܘܩܥ.
