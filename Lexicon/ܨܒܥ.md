## Forms

The conjugation of a strong triliteral verb, following the paradigm on [[Morphophonology]] and the model page [[Lexicon/ܡܠܟ]]. The citation form is the 3m past. Each cell gives the vocalized Syriac, the romanization and the plain spelling.

### Pronunciation

The third radical is a guttural (ʿ). It is written in every form, but it is silent in the coda: *ṣabaʿ* is pronounced as if *ṣaba*, *ṣabaʿtâ* as *ṣabatâ* and *ṣabʿû* as *ṣab.û*; where it begins a syllable (*ṣabaʿat*) it is pronounced. A guttural cannot be geminated, and none of these forms needs it to be.

### Imperative

The imperative is the second-person non-past with its prefix removed. Every imperative is written with an exclamation mark. Where the stem starts with a consonant cluster, the first syllable carries a schwa (*ə*), and the stress falls on the next syllable. The masculine singular is spelled like the 3m past, and the mark distinguishes it.

| | singular | plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| m | *ṣəbaʿ!* ܨܒܥ! | *ṣəbaʿnâ!* ܨܒܥܢܐ! |
| f | *ṣəbaʿî!* ܨܒܥܝ! | *ṣəbaʿnâ!* ܨܒܥܢܐ! |

### Passive

The passive changes the vowel after the first consonant to long *û*, written with a waw, so it shows in unvocalized text. Everything else follows the active paradigm. The participle and infinitive have no vowel after the first consonant to change (*ma-* and *la-* are prefixes), so they change the second vowel instead: *maṣbûʿ-* and *laṣbûʿ*. The imperative has no passive.
