### Definitions

- **G** *ṣabaʿ* ܨܒܥ: to paint, to dye.
- **Gp** *ṣûbaʿ* ܨܘܒܥ: passive of G: to be painted, to be dyed.
- **D** *ṣîbbaʿ* ܨܝܒܥ: to tint, to paint in detail, to colour thoroughly.
- **Dp** *ṣûbbaʿ* ܨܘܒܥ: passive of D: to be tinted.
- **tD** *tiṣîbbaʿ* ܬܨܝܒܥ: to take on colour, to be dyed, to put on make-up.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܨܒܥ; Gp ܨܘܒܥ; D ܨܝܒܥ; Dp ܨܘܒܥ; tD ܬܨܝܒܥ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
