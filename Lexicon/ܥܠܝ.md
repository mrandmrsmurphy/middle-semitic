### Definitions

- **G** *ʿalê* ܥܠܝ: to ascend, to go up.
- **Gp** *ʿûlê* ܥܘܠܝ: passive of G: to be ascended, to be climbed.
- **C** *šaʿlê* ܫܥܠܝ: to raise, to bring up, to offer up.
- **Cp** *šûʿlê* ܫܘܥܠܝ: passive of C: to be raised, to be offered up.
- **tD** *tiʿîllê* ܬܥܝܠܝ: to rise above, to be exalted.
- This verb has no D, Dp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܥܠܝ; Gp ܥܘܠܝ; C ܫܥܠܝ; Cp ܫܘܥܠܝ; tD ܬܥܝܠܝ.
