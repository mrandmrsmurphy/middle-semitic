### Definitions

- **G** *ʿabar* ܥܒܪ: to cross, to traverse, to pass over.
- **Gp** *ʿûbar* ܥܘܒܪ: passive of G: to be crossed.
- **D** *ʿîbbar* ܥܝܒܪ: to convey, to express, to interpret.
- **Dp** *ʿûbbar* ܥܘܒܪ: passive of D: to be conveyed, to be expressed.
- **C** *šaʿbar* ܫܥܒܪ: to transfer, to bring across, to pass on.
- **Cp** *šûʿbar* ܫܘܥܒܪ: passive of C: to be transferred.
- **tD** *tiʿîbbar* ܬܥܝܒܪ: to find expression, to be interpreted.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܥܒܪ; Gp ܥܘܒܪ; D ܥܝܒܪ; Dp ܥܘܒܪ; C ܫܥܒܪ; Cp ܫܘܥܒܪ; tD ܬܥܝܒܪ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
