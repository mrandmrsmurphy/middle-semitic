## Forms

Numeral 6, with chiastic agreement: the numeral takes the opposite gender from its noun, so the lexical form (adjective style, without the feminine marker) goes with a feminine noun and the form in *-atâ* (built like a feminine singular noun) goes with a masculine noun. The counted noun is plural. The bare counting form is used only when counting pure numbers, as in arithmetic or on the fingers; it keeps the *-â* where the bare form would end in an illegal cluster. See [[Morphosyntax]].
