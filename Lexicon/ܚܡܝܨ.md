## Forms

The conjugation of a stative verb, following the paradigm on [[Morphophonology]]. The citation form is the 3m past. Each cell gives the vocalized Syriac, the romanization and the plain spelling. The non-past is *yaḥmîṣ*, so the stative *î* shows in every form.

### Past

Stative verb: the vowel *î* is the second vowel of the stem and stays in every finite form; there is no passive.

### Imperative

The imperative is the second-person non-past with its prefix removed. Every imperative is written with an exclamation mark. Where the stem starts with a consonant cluster, the first syllable carries a schwa (*ə*), and the stress falls on the next syllable. The masculine singular is spelled like the 3m past, and the mark distinguishes it.

| | singular | plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| m | *ḥəmîṣ!* ܚܡܝܨ! | *ḥəmîṣnâ!* ܚܡܝܨܢܐ! |
| f | *ḥəmîṣî!* ܚܡܝܨܝ! | *ḥəmîṣnâ!* ܚܡܝܨܢܐ! |
