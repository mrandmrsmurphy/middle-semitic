### Definitions

- **G** *ḥamîṣ* ܚܡܝܨ: to be sour.
- **D** *ḥîmmaṣ* ܚܝܡܨ: to make sour, to acidify.
- **Dp** *ḥûmmaṣ* ܚܘܡܨ: passive of D: to be made sour.
- **tD** *tiḥîmmaṣ* ܬܚܝܡܨ: to go sour, to become embittered.
- This verb has no Gp, C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܡܝܨ; D ܚܝܡܨ; Dp ܚܘܡܨ; tD ܬܚܝܡܨ.
