### Definitions

- **G** *badâ* ܒܕܐ: to invent, to originate.
- **Gp** *bûdâ* ܒܘܕܐ: passive of G: to be invented.
- **D** *bîddâ* ܒܝܕܐ: to innovate, to devise anew.
- **Dp** *bûddâ* ܒܘܕܐ: passive of D: to be innovated.
- **C** *šabdâ* ܫܒܕܐ: to bring into being, to initiate.
- **Cp** *šûbdâ* ܫܘܒܕܐ: passive of C: to be initiated.
- **tD** *tibîddâ* ܬܒܝܕܐ: to arise, to come into being.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܒܕܐ; Gp ܒܘܕܐ; D ܒܝܕܐ; Dp ܒܘܕܐ; C ܫܒܕܐ; Cp ܫܘܒܕܐ; tD ܬܒܝܕܐ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
