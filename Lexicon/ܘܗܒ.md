### Definitions

- **G** *wahab* ܘܗܒ: to give.
- **Gp** *wûhab* ܘܘܗܒ: passive of G: to be given.
- This verb has no D, Dp, C, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܘܗܒ; Gp ܘܘܗܒ.
