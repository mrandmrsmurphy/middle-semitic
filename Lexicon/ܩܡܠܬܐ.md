| Notes | This word is the worked example of the broadest-root rule in [[Design Philosophy]]: the root Q-M-L runs across the region and down to Proto-Semitic, so Middle Semitic takes it even though everyday Hebrew moved on to כִּנָּה. The chain: Akkadian *kalmatu* "insect, bug" → Aramaic ܩܠܡܐ → Arabic قَمْل "lice" (a metathesis; collective, singulative قَمْلَة), with قُمَّل in Qur'an 7:133, the plague of lice on Egypt. The Hebrew trace קַלְמָנָה is Rabbinic (Jastrow). No collective is coined; the word is an ordinary countable. Ugaritic and the exact Proto-Semitic form not checked. |

## Forms

True feminine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]].
