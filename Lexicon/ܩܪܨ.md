### Definitions

- **G** *qaraṣ* ܩܪܨ: to pinch, to poke.
- **Gp** *qûraṣ* ܩܘܪܨ: passive of G: to be pinched.
- **D** *qîrraṣ* ܩܝܪܨ: to pinch repeatedly, to nip.
- **Dp** *qûrraṣ* ܩܘܪܨ: passive of D: to be nipped.
- **tD** *tiqîrraṣ* ܬܩܝܪܨ: to nip at each other (assumes a plural subject).
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܩܪܨ; Gp ܩܘܪܨ; D ܩܝܪܨ; Dp ܩܘܪܨ; tD ܬܩܝܪܨ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
