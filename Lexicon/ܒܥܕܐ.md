| Notes | Distance, the far place or interval. Arabic بُعْد *buʿd* is the verbal noun of *baʿuda* \"be distant\" (\"distance, interval\"); Hebrew בַּעַד is \"properly a noun denoting distance\" (Klein). The preposition is [[Lexicon/ܒܥܕ|ܒܥܕ]] *baʿad* \"after\". The form follows the regular masculine template (*baʿdâ*, like [[Lexicon/ܬܚܬܐ|ܬܚܬܐ]]); the coda *ʿ* is written and not pronounced. |

## Forms

Masculine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
