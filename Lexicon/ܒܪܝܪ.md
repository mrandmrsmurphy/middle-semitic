### Definitions

- **G** *barîr* ܒܪܝܪ: to be pious, to be righteous.
- **D** *bîrrar* ܒܝܪܪ: to purify, to make righteous.
- **Dp** *bûrrar* ܒܘܪܪ: passive of D: to be purified.
- **C** *šabrar* ܫܒܪܪ: to treat dutifully, to honor.
- **Cp** *šûbrar* ܫܘܒܪܪ: passive of C: to be treated dutifully, to be honored.
- **tD** *tibîrrar* ܬܒܝܪܪ: to be purified, to prove oneself pious.
- This verb has no Gp stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܒܪܝܪ; D ܒܝܪܪ; Dp ܒܘܪܪ; C ܫܒܪܪ; Cp ܫܘܒܪܪ; tD ܬܒܝܪܪ.
