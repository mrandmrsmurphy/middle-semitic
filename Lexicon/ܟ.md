## Forms

One of the eight small prepositions that take the pronominal suffixes (see [[Middle Semitic/Morphosyntax]]). The inflected forms exist as words of their own:

| | form | gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1cs | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܟܢܝ\|ܟܢܝ]] *kinî* | like me |
| 2ms | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܟܟܐ\|ܟܟܐ]] *kikâ* | like you (m) |
| 2fs | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܟܟܝ\|ܟܟܝ]] *kikî* | like you (f) |
| 3ms | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܟܗܘ\|ܟܗܘ]] *kihû* | like him |
| 3fs | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܟܗܐ\|ܟܗܐ]] *kihâ* | like her |
| 1cp | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܟܢܘ\|ܟܢܘ]] *kinû* | like us |
| 2cp | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܟܟܘܡ\|ܟܟܘܡ]] *kikûm* | like y'all |
| 3cp | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܟܗܘܡ\|ܟܗܘܡ]] *kihûm* | like them |

### Fast speech

A clitic: it attaches to the following word and takes no stress. In fast speech it is acceptable to say it with a schwa for its vowel, as though *ki-* were *kə-*: *kəMadrid* = like Madrid (for *kiMadrid*). The schwa is marginal elsewhere; see [[Middle Semitic/Phonology]].
