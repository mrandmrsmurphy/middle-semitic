## Forms

True feminine declension (the *t* of the stem shows in the inflected forms, as in Hebrew נוֹצַת־). There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]].
