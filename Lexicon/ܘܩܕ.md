### Definitions

- **G** *waqad* ܘܩܕ: to burn, to be on fire.
- **C** *šôqad* ܫܘܩܕ: to kindle, to set alight.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܘܩܕ; C ܫܘܩܕ.
