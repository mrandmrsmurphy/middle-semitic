| Notes | The *ta-* noun of [[Lexicon/ܙܟܪ]] “remember.” It can name a reminder that prompts recall, a memorial that preserves a memory, or an observance that commemorates someone or something. Arabic *taḏkira* supplies the *ta-* pattern and the reminder sense. Hebrew *azkārā* parallels the commemorative sense. The G infinitive *lazkar* names the act of remembering.
