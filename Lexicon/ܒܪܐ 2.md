### Definitions

- **G** *barâ* ܒܪܐ: to create (ex nihilo).
- **Gp** *bûrâ* ܒܘܪܐ: passive of G: to be created.
- **D** *bîrrâ* ܒܝܪܐ: to clear, to absolve.
- **Dp** *bûrrâ* ܒܘܪܐ: passive of D: to be cleared, to be absolved.
- **C** *šabrâ* ܫܒܪܐ: to heal, to cure.
- **Cp** *šûbrâ* ܫܘܒܪܐ: passive of C: to be healed.
- This verb has no tD stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܒܪܐ; Gp ܒܘܪܐ; D ܒܝܪܐ; Dp ܒܘܪܐ; C ܫܒܪܐ; Cp ܫܘܒܪܐ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
