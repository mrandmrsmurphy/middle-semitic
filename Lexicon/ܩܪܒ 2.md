| Notes | Near, close to, by. Arabic قُرْبَ *qurba* "near, in the vicinity of" is the adverbial accusative of the noun قُرْب *qurb* "nearness, presence" (verbal noun of *qaruba* "be near"), so it is the noun used as a place word, like *taḥat*; Hebrew has the adjective קָרוֹב *qārôb* + *lə-* (the MS adjective [[Lexicon/ܩܪܝܒܐ|ܩܪܝܒܐ]] *qarîbâ*) and the noun קֶרֶב *qerev* "inward part, midst" (*bĕqerev* "in the midst of"), without a preposition for "near"; Aramaic ܩܪܒ and Ugaritic *qrb* have the verb. Branch vote: Q-R-B on every branch (Hebrew, Aramaic, Ugaritic, Arabic), ruling by the root; the competing words are not used for "near": Hebrew אֵצֶל *ʾēṣel* (ʾ-Ṣ-L "side"), Arabic عِنْدَ *ʿinda* (ʿ-N-D, also "have"), لَدَى *ladā*, بِجَانِبِ *bi-jānib* (J-N-B "side"). A long preposition: a free word with its own stress, suffixes straight onto the lexical form (*qu.ˈrab.nî*), like [[Lexicon/ܬܚܬ|ܬܚܬ]] *taḥat*, with an epenthetic *a* in the Latin row to keep the syllables CV.CVC; it is a homograph of the verb [[Lexicon/ܩܪܒ|ܩܪܒ]] *qarab* "to come near". Ugaritic not checked as a preposition; the Proto-Semitic form is not given |
