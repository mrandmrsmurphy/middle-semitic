| Notes | After (time, sequence): the preposition of the noun [[Lexicon/ܒܥܕܐ|ܒܥܕܐ]] "distance". Arabic بَعْدَ *baʿda* "after" is the adverbial accusative of *buʿd* "distance" (from *baʿuda* "be distant"), antonym of قَبْلَ *qabla*; Hebrew has בַּעַד *baʿad*, "properly a noun denoting distance" (Klein), used as the preposition "behind, around, through, for the sake of" and never "after"; Ugaritic *bʿd* "behind, through, for" (from memory, not checked). Sense split: B-ʿ-D carries "after" and [[Lexicon/ܐܚܪ|ܐܚܪ]] *ʾaḥar* carries "behind". Branch vote: Arabic B-ʿ-D with this exact sense, Hebrew B-ʿ-D as a place word of the same origin; Aramaic and outgroups not checked. A long preposition: free word with its own stress, suffixes straight onto the lexical form (*ba.ˈʿad.nî*), like [[Lexicon/ܬܚܬ|ܬܚܬ]] *taḥat*, with an epenthetic *a* in the Latin row to keep the syllables CV.CVC |
