| Notes | General word for footwear, including sandals. Hebrew and Arabic use this family for Moses’ footwear in Exodus 3:5 and Quran 20:12. The Syriac cognate is recorded in the horseshoe sense by BDB; the Peshitta in Exodus 3:5 uses ܡܣܐܢܐ. The Ugaritic reading *nʿl* “sandals” is disputed. Feminine agreement follows Hebrew and Arabic. For an open sandal specifically, see [[Lexicon/ܣܢܕܠܐ]]. |

## Forms

Crypto-feminine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
