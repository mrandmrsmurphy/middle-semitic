| Notes | Without. The preposition *bi-* plus the negator [[Lexicon/ܠܐ|ܠܐ]] *lâ* "not", fused: "in-not". Hebrew בְּלֹא *bəlō* "without" (*bə-* + *lō*), Syriac ܒܠܐ *blā* (from memory) and Arabic بِلَا *bilā* (*bi-* + *lā*) are the same compound, so every branch builds it this way. Unlike Hebrew and Arabic, MS lets it take the pronominal suffixes straight onto the lexical form (*bi.ˈlâ.nî* "without me"), where they need another word (Hebrew *bil'adāy*, Arabic *min dūnī*); in this it counts as a long preposition, though it is not a place word. The stress falls on *lâ*, since the clitic *bi-* is never stressed. The competing words are not used: Hebrew/Syriac *bil'adê* / *blaʿad* (B-L + [[Lexicon/ܥܕ|ܥܕ]] *ʿad*; NW only), Arabic *dūna* (D-W-N) and *ġayr* (Ġ-Y-R) (Arabic only), and Ḥ-S-R "lack" (a verb and noun, not a preposition). Ugaritic *bl* and Akkadian *balu(m)* "without" are from memory, not checked |
