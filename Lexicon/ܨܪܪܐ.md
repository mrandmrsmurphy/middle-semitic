## Forms

Adjective: it agrees with its noun in gender and number, and has the four forms below (masculine *-â*, feminine *-tâ*, plural *-în* and *-ût*). The final double consonant simplifies before a consonant (*ṣartâ*) and returns before a vowel (*ṣarrîn*, *ṣarrût*, *ṣarrat*). The construct forms are frozen and an archaism. A crypto-feminine noun takes the feminine forms. See [[Morphophonology]].
