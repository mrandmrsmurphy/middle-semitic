### Definitions

- **G** *patal* ܦܬܠ: to twist, to plait, to twine.
- **Gp** *pûtal* ܦܘܬܠ: passive of G: to be twisted.
- **D** *pîttal* ܦܝܬܠ: to twist tightly, to twine thoroughly.
- **Dp** *pûttal* ܦܘܬܠ: passive of D: to be twisted tightly.
- **tD** *tipîttal* ܬܦܝܬܠ: to writhe, to wriggle, to twist oneself.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܦܬܠ; Gp ܦܘܬܠ; D ܦܝܬܠ; Dp ܦܘܬܠ; tD ܬܦܝܬܠ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
