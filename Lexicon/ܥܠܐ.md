| Notes | The place that is above: the upper side, the top. It is the noun form of the preposition [[Lexicon/ܥܠ-\|ܥܠ-]] *ʿal-* "on, over, above", used when that preposition follows another one (*min ʿalâ* "from above", like *min taḥtâ*; Hebrew מִמַּעַל *mimmaʿal*, מֵעַל *mēʿal*, Syriac ܡܢ ܠܥܠ). Hebrew מַעַל *maʿal* is "the upper part, above" (Klein, BDB), the Aramaic noun is ܥܠܐ *ʿellā* "the position above" (Wiktionary, Neo-Aramaic), and Arabic عُلُوّ *ʿuluww* is "height, elevation". The form is the plain stem + *-â* (*ʿalâ*), without the *y* of the III-yod root; it is a homograph of *ʿa-* + *lâ* "not", which the grammar does not produce. |

## Forms

Masculine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
