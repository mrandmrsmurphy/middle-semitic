| Notes | Collective noun: the falling mass of hail, not one hailstone. All three source branches agree on the word: Hebrew בָּרָד, Aramaic בַּרְדָא (ܒܪܕܐ) and Arabic بَرَد (collective; the singulative بَرَدَة is the single stone). The root also means "to be cold" in Arabic (بَرَدَ, بَارِد, the modern word for cold) and Ge'ez (*brud*); MS gives "to be cold" to [[Lexicon/ܩܪܝܪ\|ܩܪܝܪ]] and keeps this root for hail. Derived from PS *barad-* by the consonant chart on [[Anthropology]]: all radicals are regular. Ugaritic not checked. |

## Forms

Collective noun: it is not counted and forms no plural.
