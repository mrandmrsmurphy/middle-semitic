Hebrew רָבַב Qal is "be or become many, much, great" (Klein; BDB has the main Hebrew verb for "multiply", רָבָה, from R-B-Y, with Hiphil הִרְבָּה "make many", the model for the C stem), Syriac ܪܒܒ and Ge'ez ረበ are the same root; Arabic رَبَا "grow, increase" (Wiktionary compares Hebrew רָבַב; its root is R-B-W) and رَبَّ "rear, raise" are close, and the ordinary Arabic word for "multiply" is a different root, كَثُرَ / تَكَاثَرَ. Wiktionary gives PS *rabb-* as a verb "expand, extend" beside the noun "lord" and the adjective "copious", with Akkadian *rapāšum* "be wide". The stative î is the second vowel, as in [[Lexicon/ܩܪܝܪ\|ܩܪܝܪ]]; a geminate root conjugates as a strong one. ### Definitions

- **G** *rabîb* ܪܒܝܒ: to become many, to multiply, to increase.
- **C** *šarbab* ܫܪܒܒ: to make many, to multiply (transitive).
- **Cp** *šûrbab* ܫܘܪܒܒ: passive of C: to be made many.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܪܒܝܒ; C ܫܪܒܒ; Cp ܫܘܪܒܒ.
