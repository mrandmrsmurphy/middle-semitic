## Forms

Crypto-feminine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The template is *wawâ*; in the singular *a* + *w* makes *ô* and the final *â* drops in the resulting hiatus, so the lexical form is *wô*.
