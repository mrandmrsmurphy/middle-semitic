### Definitions

- **G** *ṭamâ* ܛܡܐ: to thirst, to be thirsty.
- **D** *ṭîmmâ* ܛܝܡܐ: to make thirsty, to parch.
- **Dp** *ṭûmmâ* ܛܘܡܐ: passive of D: to be made thirsty.
- This verb has no Gp, C, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܛܡܐ; D ܛܝܡܐ; Dp ܛܘܡܐ.
