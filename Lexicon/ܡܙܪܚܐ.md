| Notes | Built as m- + the root of [[Lexicon/ܙܪܚ\|ܙܪܚ]] "to shine, to rise (of the sun)", on the model of Hebrew מִזְרָח "east" (mi- + ḏ-r-ḥ, "the place the sun rises"), with the regular nominal -â; every branch forms its "east" as m- + its sunrise verb (Hebrew מִזְרָח, Arabic مَشْرِق, Syriac ܡܕܢܚܐ). Arabic مَشْرِق and شَرْق "east" are from the different root ش-ر-ق (see the Notes of [[Lexicon/ܙܪܚ\|ܙܪܚ]]), so Arabic has no cognate of this word. Aramaic ܡܕܢܚܐ "east" is recalled from the Peshitta, unverified. Masculine in all three branches. |

## Forms

Masculine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
