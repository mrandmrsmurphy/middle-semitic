| Notes | The adjective of the stative [[Lexicon/ܒܗܪ\|ܒܗܪ]] "to be bright" (the stative stem + -â). Hebrew בָּהִיר "bright, brilliant" (BDB: only Jb 37:21, a hapax; modern "bright, clear, lucid", Klein); Arabic بَاهِر "dazzling, brilliant" (participle of بَهَرَ). Jewish Aramaic בְּהִירָא is "shining, bright", but Syriac ܒܗܝܪܐ drifted to "dim, uncertain" (Klein); MS follows the Hebrew and Arabic sense. |

## Forms

Adjective: it agrees with its noun in gender and number, and has the four forms below (masculine *-â*, feminine *-tâ*, plural *-în* and *-ût*). The construct forms are frozen and an archaism. A crypto-feminine noun takes the feminine forms. See [[Morphophonology]].
