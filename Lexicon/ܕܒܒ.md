| Notes | The slow, quiet, close-to-the-ground advance of one creature: to creep, crawl, go on all fours, steal in. Arabic دَبَّ is "creep, crawl; advance slowly; go on all fours; steal in; spread, pervade, invade" (Wiktionary), the verb of the Arabic Bible's Gen 1:26 and 1:28 (Smith–Van Dyck يَدِبُّ), with the verbal noun دَبِيب and the noun دَابَّة "creature, mount". Hebrew דָּבַב is "glide, flow; murmur, speak low" (Jastrow; Song 7:10) with the noun דִּבָּה "whispering, evil report" ("that which glides stealthily", BDB), and Aramaic דְּבַב "murmur" (Jastrow); the common sense is the soft, unseen movement. The root also holds [[Lexicon/ܕܘܒܐ\|ܕܘܒܐ]] "bear", a separate Proto-Semitic noun (*dubb-*, Wiktionary), spelled alike. Branch vote: NW (Hebrew, Aramaic) D-B-B "glide, murmur"; Arabic D-B-B "creep"; Z-Ḥ-L / R-Ḥ-Š / R-M-Ś for the crawl and swarm; ruling by the sense split: D-B-B takes the single creature's creep and stealth, [[Lexicon/ܪܡܫ\|ܪܡܫ]] the many-limbed swarming creep. Akkadian, Ugaritic and Ge'ez not checked. |

### Definitions

- **G** *dabab* ܕܒܒ: to creep, to crawl, to advance stealthily, to steal in.
- **D** *dîbbab* ܕܝܒܒ: to creep about, to spread, to pervade.
- **C** *šadbab* ܫܕܒܒ: to bring in stealthily, to infiltrate.
- **Cp** *šûdbab* ܫܘܕܒܒ: passive of C: to be brought in stealthily.
- This verb has no Gp, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܕܒܒ; D ܕܝܒܒ; C ܫܕܒܒ; Cp ܫܘܕܒܒ.
