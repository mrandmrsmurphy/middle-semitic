### Definitions

- **G** *napaḥ* ܢܦܚ: to blow, to exhale, to breathe.
- **Gp** *nûpaḥ* ܢܘܦܚ: passive of G: to be blown.
- **D** *nîppaḥ* ܢܝܦܚ: to inflate, to blow up, to puff up.
- **Dp** *nûppaḥ* ܢܘܦܚ: passive of D: to be inflated.
- **tD** *tinîppaḥ* ܬܢܝܦܚ: to swell up, to puff up.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܢܦܚ; Gp ܢܘܦܚ; D ܢܝܦܚ; Dp ܢܘܦܚ; tD ܬܢܝܦܚ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
