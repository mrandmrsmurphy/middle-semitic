### Definitions

- **G** *naʿam* ܢܥܡ: to please.
- **Gp** *nûʿam* ܢܘܥܡ: passive of G: to be pleased, to be delighted.
- **D** *nîʿâm* ܢܝܥܐܡ: to pamper, to indulge.
- **Dp** *nûʿâm* ܢܘܥܐܡ: passive of D: to be pampered.
- **tD** *tinîʿâm* ܬܢܝܥܐܡ: to enjoy comfort, to live in ease.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܢܥܡ; Gp ܢܘܥܡ; D ܢܝܥܐܡ; Dp ܢܘܥܐܡ; tD ܬܢܝܥܐܡ.
