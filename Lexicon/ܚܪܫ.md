### Definitions

- **G** *ḥaraš* ܚܪܫ: to plow.
- **Gp** *ḥûraš* ܚܘܪܫ: passive of G: to be plowed.
- **D** *ḥîrraš* ܚܝܪܫ: to plow thoroughly, to break up (a field).
- **Dp** *ḥûrraš* ܚܘܪܫ: passive of D: to be broken up.
- **C** *šaḥraš* ܫܚܪܫ: to set (someone) to plowing, to have (a field) plowed.
- **Cp** *šûḥraš* ܫܘܚܪܫ: passive of C: to be set to plowing.
- This verb has no tD stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܪܫ; Gp ܚܘܪܫ; D ܚܝܪܫ; Dp ܚܘܪܫ; C ܫܚܪܫ; Cp ܫܘܚܪܫ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
