## Forms

One of the eight small prepositions that take the pronominal suffixes (see [[Middle Semitic/Morphosyntax]]). The inflected forms exist as words of their own:

| | form | gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1cs | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܒܢܝ 2\|ܒܢܝ 2]] *binî* | on me |
| 2ms | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܒܟܐ\|ܒܟܐ]] *bikâ* | on you (m) |
| 2fs | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܒܟܝ\|ܒܟܝ]] *bikî* | on you (f) |
| 3ms | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܒܗܘ\|ܒܗܘ]] *bihû* | on him |
| 3fs | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܒܗܐ\|ܒܗܐ]] *bihâ* | on her |
| 1cp | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܒܢܘ\|ܒܢܘ]] *binû* | on us |
| 2cp | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܒܟܘܡ\|ܒܟܘܡ]] *bikûm* | on y'all |
| 3cp | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܒܗܘܡ\|ܒܗܘܡ]] *bihûm* | on them |

### Fast speech

A clitic: it attaches to the following word and takes no stress. In fast speech it is acceptable to say it with a schwa for its vowel, as though *bi-* were *bə-*: *bəMadrid* = at Madrid (for *biMadrid*). The schwa is marginal elsewhere; see [[Middle Semitic/Phonology]].
