### Definitions

- **G** *salaq* ܣܠܩ: to boil (food), to blanch.
- **Gp** *sûlaq* ܣܘܠܩ: passive of G: to be boiled.
- **D** *sîllaq* ܣܝܠܩ: to boil thoroughly, to parboil.
- **Dp** *sûllaq* ܣܘܠܩ: passive of D: to be parboiled.
- **C** *šaslaq* ܫܣܠܩ: to have (something) boiled.
- **Cp** *šûslaq* ܫܘܣܠܩ: passive of C: to be had boiled.
- **tD** *tisîllaq* ܬܣܝܠܩ: to be boiled through, to come to a boil.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܣܠܩ; Gp ܣܘܠܩ; D ܣܝܠܩ; Dp ܣܘܠܩ; C ܫܣܠܩ; Cp ܫܘܣܠܩ; tD ܬܣܝܠܩ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
