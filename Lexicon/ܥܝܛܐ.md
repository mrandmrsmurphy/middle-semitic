| Notes | Advice or a course of action proposed by someone. The Arabic cognate is a verb meaning “exhort, admonish.” Branch vote: NW (Hebrew Y-ʿ-Ṣ and Aramaic Y-ʿ-Ṭ) supports advice and counsel; Arabic W-ʿ-Ẓ supports exhortation; ruling by the shared family and the attested Northwest Semitic noun. The Targum of Job 22:18 uses this family for “the counsel of the wicked,” as in [[Tanakh#Psalm 1:1]].
