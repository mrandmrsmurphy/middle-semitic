### Definitions

- **G** *bêt* ܒܝܬ: to overnight, to stay one night at.
- **Gp** *bût* ܒܘܬ: passive of G: to be stayed at overnight.
- **D** *bîyyat* ܒܝܝܬ: to lodge at, to stay repeated nights at.
- **Dp** *bûyyat* ܒܘܝܬ: passive of D: to be lodged at.
- **C** *šabyat* ܫܒܝܬ: to host, to provide overnight lodging for.
- **Cp** *šûbyat* ܫܘܒܝܬ: passive of C: to be hosted.
- **tD** *tibîyyat* ܬܒܝܝܬ: to come to have a home, to find lodging.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܒܝܬ; Gp ܒܘܬ; D ܒܝܝܬ; Dp ܒܘܝܬ; C ܫܒܝܬ; Cp ܫܘܒܝܬ; tD ܬܒܝܝܬ.
