| Notes | The image that is worshipped or kept: a cult image, an idol, a household god, a carved figure. Hebrew צֶלֶם is "image, idol, semblance" (it is also the word of Gen 1:26–27), Syriac ܨܠܡܐ "image, picture, statue, idol", Akkadian *ṣalmu* "statue, image", Ugaritic *ṣlm* "god-image, statue"; Arabic صَنَم "idol" has *n* for *l*. Branch vote: NW (Hebrew, Aramaic, Ugaritic) Ṣ-L-M; Akkadian Ṣ-L-M; Arabic Ṣ-N-M "idol"; ruling by splitting senses: Ṣ-L-M keeps the cult image, [[Lexicon/ܨܘܪܬܐ\|ܨܘܪܬܐ]] takes "form, image, picture" (so the image of God is *ṣûrtâ*), [[Lexicon/ܕܡܝ\|ܕܡܝ]] the effigy and doll, [[Lexicon/ܡܫܠܐ\|ܡܫܠܐ]] the simile and representation. |
