## Forms

Crypto-feminine declension, with the dual as for a body part. The construct state is frozen and an archaism, but it is the natural form on this word because of the many ancient texts.

| | singular | dual | plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| regular | *yidnâ* ܝܕܐ | *yidnîm* ܝܕܝܡ | *yidnût* ܝܕܘܬ |
| *construct* | *yidnat* ܝܕܬ | *yidnû* ܝܕܘ | *yidnû* ܝܕܘ |

### With pronominal suffixes

The pronominal suffixes attach to the construct state, with nothing dropped. The dual and plural constructs are the same, so one column serves both.

| | singular noun | dual or plural noun |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1sg | *yidnatnî* ܝܕܬܢܝ 'my hand' | *yidnûnî* ܝܕܘܢܝ 'my hands' |
