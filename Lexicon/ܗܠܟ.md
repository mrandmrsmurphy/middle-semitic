### Imperative

The imperative is the second-person non-past with its prefix removed. Every imperative is written with an exclamation mark. Where the stem starts with a consonant cluster, the first syllable carries a schwa (*ə*), and the stress falls on the next syllable. The masculine singular is spelled like the 3m past, and the mark distinguishes it.

| | singular | plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| m | *həlak!* ܗܠܟ! | *həlaknâ!* ܗܠܟܢܐ! |
| f | *həlakî!* ܗܠܟܝ! | *həlaknâ!* ܗܠܟܢܐ! |

### Passive

The passive changes the vowel after the first consonant to long *û*, written with a waw, so it shows in unvocalized text. Everything else follows the active paradigm. The participle and infinitive have no vowel after the first consonant to change (*ma-* and *la-* are prefixes), so they change the second vowel instead: *mahlûk-* and *lahlûk*. The imperative has no passive.
