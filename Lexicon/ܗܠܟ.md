### Definitions

- **G** *halak* ܗܠܟ: to go away, to depart, to perish.
- **D** *hîllak* ܗܝܠܟ: to walk about, to go back and forth.
- **C** *šahlak* ܫܗܠܟ: to lead away, to take away, to destroy.
- **Cp** *šûhlak* ܫܘܗܠܟ: passive of C: to be led away, to be destroyed.
- **tD** *tihîllak* ܬܗܝܠܟ: to walk about, to conduct oneself.
- This verb has no Gp, Dp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܗܠܟ; D ܗܝܠܟ; C ܫܗܠܟ; Cp ܫܘܗܠܟ; tD ܬܗܝܠܟ.
