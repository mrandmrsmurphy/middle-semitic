| Notes | Hebrew חוֹל and Aramaic ܚܠܐ "sand, gravel" are the word; Arabic has no cognate from this root (its sand is رَمْل, attested from Old South Arabian). The diphthong contracts (*ḥawlâ* → *ḥôlâ*), as in [[Lexicon/ܚܘܡܐ\|ܚܘܡܐ]] *ḥômâ* "dark". A mass noun in all three sources; one grain of sand is the diminutive [[Lexicon/ܚܘܠܘܢܐ\|ܚܘܠܘܢܐ]] *ḥôlōnâ*. A Ugaritic *ḥl* "sand" is recalled but unverified; Proto-Semitic not checked. |

## Forms

Masculine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
