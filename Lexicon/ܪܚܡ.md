### Definitions

- **G** *raḥam* ܪܚܡ: to have mercy on, to pity.
- **Gp** *rûḥam* ܪܘܚܡ: passive of G: to be shown mercy.
- **D** *rîḥâm* ܪܝܚܐܡ: to plead for mercy for, to invoke mercy on.
- **Dp** *rûḥâm* ܪܘܚܐܡ: passive of D: to be pleaded for.
- **C** *šarḥam* ܫܪܚܡ: to move to pity, to make merciful.
- **Cp** *šûrḥam* ܫܘܪܚܡ: passive of C: to be moved to pity.
- **tD** *tirîḥâm* ܬܪܝܚܐܡ: to show mercy to one another (assumes a plural subject).

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܪܚܡ; Gp ܪܘܚܡ; D ܪܝܚܐܡ; Dp ܪܘܚܐܡ; C ܫܪܚܡ; Cp ܫܘܪܚܡ; tD ܬܪܝܚܐܡ.
