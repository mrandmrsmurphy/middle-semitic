### Definitions

- **G** *sabaṭ* ܣܒܛ: to beat, to flog.
- **Gp** *sûbaṭ* ܣܘܒܛ: passive of G: to be beaten.
- **D** *sîbbaṭ* ܣܝܒܛ: to thrash, to beat repeatedly.
- **Dp** *sûbbaṭ* ܣܘܒܛ: passive of D: to be thrashed.
- **C** *šasbaṭ* ܫܣܒܛ: to have (someone) beaten.
- **Cp** *šûsbaṭ* ܫܘܣܒܛ: passive of C: to be had beaten.
- **tD** *tisîbbaṭ* ܬܣܝܒܛ: to scuffle, to beat one another (assumes a plural subject).

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܣܒܛ; Gp ܣܘܒܛ; D ܣܝܒܛ; Dp ܣܘܒܛ; C ܫܣܒܛ; Cp ܫܘܣܒܛ; tD ܬܣܝܒܛ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
