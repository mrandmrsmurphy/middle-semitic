### Definitions

- **G** *ḥamad* ܚܡܕ: to desire, to covet, to delight in.
- **Gp** *ḥûmad* ܚܘܡܕ: passive of G: to be desired.
- **D** *ḥîmmad* ܚܝܡܕ: to cherish, to prize highly, to extol.
- **Dp** *ḥûmmad* ܚܘܡܕ: passive of D: to be extolled, to be prized.
- **C** *šaḥmad* ܫܚܡܕ: to make desirable.
- **Cp** *šûḥmad* ܫܘܚܡܕ: passive of C: to be made desirable.
- **tD** *tiḥîmmad* ܬܚܝܡܕ: to be desirable, to make oneself desirable.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܡܕ; Gp ܚܘܡܕ; D ܚܝܡܕ; Dp ܚܘܡܕ; C ܫܚܡܕ; Cp ܫܘܚܡܕ; tD ܬܚܝܡܕ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
