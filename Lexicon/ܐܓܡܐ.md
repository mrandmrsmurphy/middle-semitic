| Notes | A Sumerian Wanderwort: Sumerian *agam* → Akkadian *agammum* → the West Semitic languages (Wiktionary). The senses meet at wet, overgrown ground: Hebrew אֲגַם "lake; swamp, marsh; reed", Aramaic ܐܓܡܐ "marsh, swamp", Arabic أَجَمَة "jungle, brushwood". MS takes the overlap — marsh, swamp, wet jungle; open standing water is [[Lexicon/ܒܚܪܘܬܐ\|ܒܚܪܘܬܐ]] *baḥrôtâ* "lake". Hebrew's "lake" sense of אֲגַם is therefore not taken. Ugaritic not checked; a Proto-Semitic row would be wrong, since the word is a loan. |

## Forms
