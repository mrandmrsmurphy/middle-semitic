| Notes | Collective noun: the writhing mass, not one worm. A single worm is [[Lexicon/ܬܘܠܥܬܐ\|ܬܘܠܥܬܐ]] *tôlaʿatâ*. Arabic دُود is a collective whose singulative is دُودَة; the collective/singulative pattern is shared with Hebrew (compare דָּג *dag* "fish" collectively, דָּגָה *dāgâ* one fish). This is the first lexicalized collective in MS. Hebrew, Aramaic, Ugaritic and Proto-Semitic not checked. |

## Forms

Collective noun: it is not counted and forms no plural. For one worm, use [[Lexicon/ܬܘܠܥܬܐ|ܬܘܠܥܬܐ]].
