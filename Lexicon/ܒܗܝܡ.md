### Definitions

- **G** *bahîm* ܒܗܝܡ: to be confused, to be murky.
- **D** *bîhâm* ܒܝܗܐܡ: to muddle, to obscure thoroughly.
- **Dp** *bûhâm* ܒܘܗܐܡ: passive of D: to be muddled.
- **C** *šabham* ܫܒܗܡ: to confuse, to make obscure or ambiguous, to conceal.
- **Cp** *šûbham* ܫܘܒܗܡ: passive of C: to be made obscure, to be concealed.
- **tD** *tibîhâm* ܬܒܝܗܐܡ: to become confused, to be perplexed.
- This verb has no Gp stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܒܗܝܡ; D ܒܝܗܐܡ; Dp ܒܘܗܐܡ; C ܫܒܗܡ; Cp ܫܘܒܗܡ; tD ܬܒܝܗܐܡ. 

## Forms

The conjugation of a stative verb in each of its stems, from the templates in [[Morphophonology]]. The citation form is the 3m past. Each cell gives the vocalized Syriac, the romanization and the plain spelling. In the G stem the vowel *î* is the second vowel of the stem and stays in every finite form; a stative has no passive, so it has no Gp. The derived stems are those of a strong verb. The middle guttural cannot be geminated, so the D, Dp and tD stems lengthen the vowel after it instead, written with an aleph.
