### Definitions

- **G** *zavaḥ* ܙܒܚ: to slaughter, to massacre.
- **Gp** *zûvaḥ* ܙܘܒܚ: passive of G: to be slaughtered.
- **D** *zîbbaḥ* ܙܝܒܚ: to slaughter in numbers, to massacre.
- **Dp** *zûbbaḥ* ܙܘܒܚ: passive of D: to be massacred.
- **C** *šazbaḥ* ܫܙܒܚ: to have (someone) slaughter.
- **Cp** *šûzbaḥ* ܫܘܙܒܚ: passive of C: to be had slaughtered.
- **tD** *tizîbbaḥ* ܬܙܝܒܚ: to be slaughtered in numbers.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܙܒܚ; Gp ܙܘܒܚ; D ܙܝܒܚ; Dp ܙܘܒܚ; C ܫܙܒܚ; Cp ܫܘܙܒܚ; tD ܬܙܝܒܚ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
