| Notes | A ravine or pass between heights. Hebrew גַּיְא is a valley or ravine. Samaritan has a comparable form; wider cognate proposals remain uncertain. Arabic شِعْب is a translation equivalent for a ravine or pass. The MS vowel follows *ay* → *ê*, with root aleph consonantal before the noun ending. Branch vote: NW (Hebrew, Samaritan comparison) G-Y-ʾ; Arabic uses other families for this sense; outgroup evidence uncertain; ruling by the Hebrew terrain sense. ## Forms
