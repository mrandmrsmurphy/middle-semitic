### Definitions

- **G** *ḥaṣad* ܚܨܕ: to reap, to harvest, to mow.
- **Gp** *ḥûṣad* ܚܘܨܕ: passive of G: to be reaped.
- **D** *ḥîṣṣad* ܚܝܨܕ: to reap thoroughly, to harvest in quantity.
- **Dp** *ḥûṣṣad* ܚܘܨܕ: passive of D: to be harvested in quantity.
- This verb has no C, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܨܕ; Gp ܚܘܨܕ; D ܚܝܨܕ; Dp ܚܘܨܕ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
