| Notes | Throughout, during, for the length of, as long as. Arabic طَوَالَ *ṭawāla* "during, throughout, along" is the adverbial accusative of the noun طِوَال *ṭiwāl* ("length"; the noun is also *ṭūl*, as in *ṭūla l-yawm* "all day long"), so it is the noun used as a place word, like *taḥat*. It is the root of the adjective [[Lexicon/ܛܘܠܐ|ܛܘܠܐ]] *ṭôlâ* "long", so it follows the vault's choice of Ṭ-W-L over ʾ-R-K for "long". Hebrew has no Ṭ-W-L word; it uses ʾ-R-K (*ʾōrek* "length", *lĕʾōrek* "along, throughout"); Aramaic not checked. Sense split from [[Lexicon/ܥܕ|ܥܕ]] *ʿad* and *pi-*: *pi-* is the plain "in, within" (*pi-milḥamâ* style), *ʿad* is "up to", and *ṭawâl* is "all through, for the whole length of". Not used: Hebrew *bəmešekh* (M-Š-K, Hebrew only), Arabic *ḫilāla* (Ḫ-L-L), *ʾaṯnāʾa* (Ṯ-N-Y) and *muddata* (M-D-D). A long preposition: a free word with its own stress, suffixes straight onto the lexical form (*ṭa.ˈwâl.nî*), like [[Lexicon/ܬܚܬ|ܬܚܬ]] *taḥat*. The *w* is a consonant (*ṭa.wâl*), so it does not contract to *ô* as in *ṭôlâ*; the internal *â* is not written. No matching noun: the regular "length" form would be spelled ܛܘܠܐ, the same as the adjective. As a conjunction "as long as" it would be followed by *ʾan*, not yet ruled. Ugaritic and the outgroups not checked |
