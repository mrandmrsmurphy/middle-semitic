| Notes | The generic verb of making a sound with the voice, of people and of animals. Arabic قَالَ is the everyday "to say" (MSA, Egyptian *ʔāl*, Palestinian *ʔāl*); MS takes the wider sense, and gives the plain "he said" of dialogue to [[Lexicon/ܐܡܪ\|ܐܡܪ]] by the branch-vote ruling. Hebrew has no verb on this root in Sefaria's BDB and Klein, only the noun קוֹל (see [[Lexicon/ܩܘܠܐ\|ܩܘܠܐ]]); Aramaic ܩܠܐ is likewise a noun and the verb is not checked. A hollow verb: the middle w contracts (*qawal* → *qôl*), as in [[Lexicon/ܟܘܢ\|ܟܘܢ]] *kôn*. |

### Definitions

- **G** *qôl* ܩܘܠ: to sound forth, to vocalize, to make sound.
- **C** *šaqwal* ܫܩܘܠ: to give voice to, to make sound.
- **Cp** *šûqwal* ܫܘܩܘܠ: passive of C: to be given voice.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܩܘܠ; C ܫܩܘܠ; Cp ܫܘܩܘܠ.
