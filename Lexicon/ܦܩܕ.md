| Notes | To visit, to attend to, to call to account, to punish: the core sense is "take charge of, attend to, hold to account" (Exodus 20:5, God *pāqēd* the guilt of the fathers upon the sons). The same root in each language: Hebrew פָּקַד *pāqad* "attend to, visit, muster, count, appoint, remember, punish" (BDB, Klein), Arabic فَقَدَ *faqada* "lose, miss" with form VIII *iftaqada* "seek out the absent, inspect, visit" (the verb of this verse in the Smith–Van Dyck text), Aramaic פְּקַד *pəqad* "command" (the Peshitta's ܦܘܩܕܢܝ "my commandments" in Exodus 20:6), Akkadian *paqādu* "entrust" and Ugaritic *pqd* (from memory). Consonants by the chart on [[Anthropology]]: PS *p* is MS ܦ (Arabic ف). Branch vote: P-Q-D on every branch, in related senses; no competing root. The G sense is Hebrew's visiting and punishing; the Gp is Hebrew Niphal and Pual "be missed, be visited"; the C is Hebrew Hiphil "appoint, put in charge"; the D is Hebrew Piel and Aramaic "command, charge". The Arabic "lose, miss" is not given its own stem. The stems are Claude's proposal. Hebrew, Arabic and Aramaic confirmed on Wiktionary and BDB; Ugaritic and Akkadian from memory and cross-references |

### Definitions

- **G** *paqad* ܦܩܕ: to visit, to attend to, to call to account, to punish.
- **Gp** *pûqad* ܦܘܩܕ: passive of G: to be visited, to be missed.
- **D** *pîqqad* ܦܝܩܕ: to command, to charge.
- **C** *šapqad* ܫܦܩܕ: to appoint, to put in charge.
- This verb has no Dp, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܦܩܕ; Gp ܦܘܩܕ; D ܦܝܩܕ; C ܫܦܩܕ.
