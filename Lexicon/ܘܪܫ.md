| Notes | To receive property, land, or a position by inheritance or succession. The [[Anthropology#Consonant correspondences\|consonant chart]] maps inherited *ṯ* to MS *š*. Branch vote: NW (Hebrew Y-R-Š and Aramaic Y-R-T), Arabic W-R-Ṯ, and Geʿez W-R-S share “inherit”; ruling by the broadest shared root. The same family appears in [[Quran#Quran 21:105]] and the Hebrew and Syriac of Psalm 37:29.
