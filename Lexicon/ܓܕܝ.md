## Forms

Masculine declension of a noun from a III-yod root: the final *y* of the stem surfaces as *ê* in the singular and as *î* before the plural ending. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Middle Semitic/Morphophonology]].
