### Definitions

- **G** *qarâ* ܩܪܐ: to read, to call, to recite.
- **Gp** *qûrâ* ܩܘܪܐ: passive of G: to be read, to be called.
- **C** *šaqrâ* ܫܩܪܐ: to read aloud, to have (someone) read.
- **Cp** *šûqrâ* ܫܘܩܪܐ: passive of C: to be read aloud.
- This verb has no D, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܩܪܐ; Gp ܩܘܪܐ; C ܫܩܪܐ; Cp ܫܘܩܪܐ.
