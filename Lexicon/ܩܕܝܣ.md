### Definitions

- **G** *qadîs* ܩܕܝܣ: to be holy.
- **D** *qîddas* ܩܝܕܣ: to sanctify, to make holy.
- **Dp** *qûddas* ܩܘܕܣ: passive of D: to be sanctified.
- **C** *šaqdas* ܫܩܕܣ: to consecrate, to dedicate.
- **Cp** *šûqdas* ܫܘܩܕܣ: passive of C: to be consecrated, to be dedicated.
- **tD** *tiqîddas* ܬܩܝܕܣ: to be hallowed, to sanctify oneself.
- This verb has no Gp stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܩܕܝܣ; D ܩܝܕܣ; Dp ܩܘܕܣ; C ܫܩܕܣ; Cp ܫܘܩܕܣ; tD ܬܩܝܕܣ.
