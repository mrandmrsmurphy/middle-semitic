### Definitions

- **G** *šakar* ܫܟܪ: to thank, to be grateful to.
- **Gp** *šûkar* ܫܘܟܪ: passive of G: to be thanked.
- **D** *šîkkar* ܫܝܟܪ: to thank profusely, to extol.
- **Dp** *šûkkar* ܫܘܟܪ: passive of D: to be extolled.
- **tD** *tišîkkar* ܬܫܝܟܪ: to give thanks, to be grateful.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܫܟܪ; Gp ܫܘܟܪ; D ܫܝܟܪ; Dp ܫܘܟܪ; tD ܬܫܝܟܪ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
