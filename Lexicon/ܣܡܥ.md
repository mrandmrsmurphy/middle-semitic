### Definitions

- **G** *samaʿ* ܣܡܥ: to hear, to listen.
- **Gp** *sûmaʿ* ܣܘܡܥ: passive of G: to be heard.
- **D** *sîmmaʿ* ܣܝܡܥ: to recite, to proclaim.
- **Dp** *sûmmaʿ* ܣܘܡܥ: passive of D: to be recited.
- **C** *šasmaʿ* ܫܣܡܥ: to make hear, to announce.
- **Cp** *šûsmaʿ* ܫܘܣܡܥ: passive of C: to be announced.
- **tD** *tisîmmaʿ* ܬܣܝܡܥ: to listen closely, to eavesdrop.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܣܡܥ; Gp ܣܘܡܥ; D ܣܝܡܥ; Dp ܣܘܡܥ; C ܫܣܡܥ; Cp ܫܘܣܡܥ; tD ܬܣܝܡܥ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
