### Definitions

- **G** *baraq* ܒܪܩ: to flash, to lighten, to shine.
- **C** *šabraq* ܫܒܪܩ: to flash (a signal), to signal, to telegraph.
- **Cp** *šûbraq* ܫܘܒܪܩ: passive of C: to be signalled.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܒܪܩ; C ܫܒܪܩ; Cp ܫܘܒܪܩ.
