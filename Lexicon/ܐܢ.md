| Notes | Introduces a clause that is the complement of a verb of saying, knowing or perceiving, and stands for no noun: *He said that she is coming*; *I testify that there is no god* (*ašhad an lâ yiš êlhâ ...*). Compare [[Lexicon/ܠܝܕ\|ܠܝܕ]], the relative pronoun, which stands for a noun. |
| Compare | The productive adjective suffix [[Lexicon/-ܐܢ\|-ܐܢ]] (*-ân*) is spelled the same; the two are not known to be related, and neither has a root. |
