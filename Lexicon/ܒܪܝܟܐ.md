| Notes | The pool is the knee-shaped hollow on the kneel root: MS [[Lexicon/ܒܪܟ\|ܒܪܟ]] "to kneel" and [[Lexicon/ܒܪܟܐ\|ܒܪܟܐ]] *birkâ* "knee" share it. The form follows Hebrew בְּרֵיכָה (with full *a* for the Hebrew schwa), which keeps the pool apart from the blessing — Hebrew בְּרָכָה, Arabic بَرَكَة — on the same root. Aramaic, Ugaritic and Proto-Semitic not checked. |

## Forms

True feminine declension (the *t* of the stem shows in the inflected forms, as in Hebrew בְּרֵיכַת־). There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]].
