### Definitions

- **G** *wabal* ܘܒܠ: to bear, to carry along.
- **Gp** *wûbal* ܘܘܒܠ: passive of G: to be borne, to be carried.
- **C** *šôbal* ܫܘܒܠ: to lead, to conduct, to transport.
- **Cp** *šûwbal* ܫܘܘܒܠ: passive of C: to be led, to be transported.
- This verb has no D, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܘܒܠ; Gp ܘܘܒܠ; C ܫܘܒܠ; Cp ܫܘܘܒܠ.
