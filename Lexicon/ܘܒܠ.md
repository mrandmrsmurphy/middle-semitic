### Imperative

The imperative is the second-person non-past without its prefix consonant. Every imperative is written with an exclamation mark. Where the stem starts with a consonant cluster, the first syllable carries a schwa (*ə*), and the stress falls on the next syllable.

| | singular | plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| m | *ôbal!* ܘܒܠ! | *ôbalnâ!* ܘܒܠܢܐ! |
| f | *ôbalî!* ܘܒܠܝ! | *ôbalnâ!* ܘܒܠܢܐ! |

### Passive

The passive changes the vowel after the first consonant to long *û*, written with a waw. In the passive non-past the root-initial mater reverts to a consonant after the *û* (aleph as a glottal stop), which keeps the passive distinct from the active. The participle and infinitive change the second vowel. The imperative has no passive.
