| Notes | To serve, to work, to worship: the verb of the noun [[Lexicon/ܥܒܕܐ|ܥܒܕܐ]] "slave, servant" (one who serves). Hebrew עָבַד *ʿāvad* "work, serve, worship" (Exodus 20:5 *tāʿovdēm* "you shall serve them"), Arabic عَبَدَ *ʿabada* "serve, worship" and Ugaritic *ʿbd* "work, serve"; Wiktionary calls the root a Central Semitic innovation (cognate noun in Hebrew, Phoenician, Ugaritic, Syriac, Old South Arabian). Aramaic ܥܒܕ *ʿbad* is the general "do, make" (the Peshitta's word in Exodus 20:4), a sense MS gives to [[Lexicon/ܦܥܠ|ܦܥܠ]] *paʿal*. The C stem is Hebrew Hiphil *heʿĕvîd* and Arabic IV *ʾaʿbada* "enslave"; the tD is Arabic V *taʿabbada* "worship devoutly". Branch vote: Hebrew, Ugaritic and Arabic share the sense "serve, work, worship" on ʿ-B-D, so no competing root is needed (Aramaic P-L-Ḥ "serve" is Aramaic only). The stems are Claude's proposal. Hebrew and Arabic confirmed on Wiktionary and BDB; Ugaritic is from the noun's entry; outgroups not checked |

### Definitions

- **G** *ʿabad* ܥܒܕ: to serve, to work, to worship.
- **Gp** *ʿûbad* ܥܘܒܕ: passive of G: to be served, to be worshipped.
- **C** *šaʿbad* ܫܥܒܕ: to make serve, to enslave.
- **Cp** *šûʿbad* ܫܘܥܒܕ: passive of C: to be enslaved.
- **tD** *tiʿîbbad* ܬܥܝܒܕ: to worship devoutly, to devote oneself to service.
- This verb has no D, Dp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܥܒܕ; Gp ܥܘܒܕ; C ܫܥܒܕ; Cp ܫܘܥܒܕ; tD ܬܥܝܒܕ.
