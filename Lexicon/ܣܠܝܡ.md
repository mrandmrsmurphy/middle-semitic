### Definitions

- **G** *salîm* ܣܠܝܡ: to be at peace, to be safe.
- **D** *sîllam* ܣܝܠܡ: to deliver, to hand over, to greet.
- **Dp** *sûllam* ܣܘܠܡ: passive of D: to be delivered, to be handed over.
- **C** *šaslam* ܫܣܠܡ: to submit, to complete.
- **Cp** *šûslam* ܫܘܣܠܡ: passive of C: to be completed.
- **tD** *tisîllam* ܬܣܝܠܡ: to receive, to be fulfilled.
- This verb has no Gp stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܣܠܝܡ; D ܣܝܠܡ; Dp ܣܘܠܡ; C ܫܣܠܡ; Cp ܫܘܣܠܡ; tD ܬܣܝܠܡ.
