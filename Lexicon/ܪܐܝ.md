### Definitions

- **G** *raʾê* ܪܐܝ: to see, to perceive.
- **Gp** *rûʾê* ܪܘܐܝ: passive of G: to be seen.
- **D** *rîʾʾê* ܪܝܐܝ: to display, to make a show to.
- **Dp** *rûʾʾê* ܪܘܐܝ: passive of D: to be displayed.
- **C** *šarʾê* ܫܪܐܝ: to show, to let see.
- **Cp** *šûrʾê* ܫܘܪܐܝ: passive of C: to be shown.
- **tD** *tirîʾʾê* ܬܪܝܐܝ: to see each other, to appear, to be seen.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܪܐܝ; Gp ܪܘܐܝ; D ܪܝܐܝ; Dp ܪܘܐܝ; C ܫܪܐܝ; Cp ܫܘܪܐܝ; tD ܬܪܝܐܝ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
