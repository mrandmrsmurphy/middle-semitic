## Forms

Adjective: it agrees with its noun in gender and number, and has the four forms below (masculine *-â*, feminine *-tâ*, plural *-în* and *-ût*). There are no construct forms. A crypto-feminine noun takes the feminine forms. See [[Middle Semitic/Morphophonology]].
