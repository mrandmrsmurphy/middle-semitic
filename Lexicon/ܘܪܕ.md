### Definitions

- **G** *warad* ܘܪܕ: to descend, to go down.
- **C** *šôrad* ܫܘܪܕ: to bring down, to lower.
- **Cp** *šûwrad* ܫܘܘܪܕ: passive of C: to be brought down.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܘܪܕ; C ܫܘܪܕ; Cp ܫܘܘܪܕ.
