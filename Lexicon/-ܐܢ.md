| Notes | A productive suffix that takes a noun and makes an adjective, 'marked by' it; the adjective can be used as a noun for the person so marked (a substantive). Examples: [[Lexicon/ܫܘܟܪܢ\|ܫܘܟܪܢ]] (from the verb ܫܟܪ, 'thanks!', which has become an interjection because it does not end in a long a), [[Lexicon/ܒܬܘܠܐܢܐ\|ܒܬܘܠܐܢܐ]] (virgin → pure, chaste), [[Lexicon/ܪܒܐܢܐ\|ܪܒܐܢܐ]] (great → a lot), and [[Lexicon/ܚܛܝܐܢܐ\|ܚܛܝܐܢܐ]] (sin → sinful, and substantively 'a sinner'). |
| Compare | The complementizer [[Lexicon/ܐܢ\|ܐܢ]] (*an*) is spelled the same; the two are not known to be related, and neither has a root. |
