| Notes | The place across: the other side, the far bank, the region beyond. Hebrew עֵבֶר *ʿēver* is exactly this noun ("the region across or beyond, the other side"), Neo-Aramaic ܥܸܒ݂ܪܵܐ *ʿiḇrā* "crossing, passage; far bank", and Arabic عَبْر *ʿabr* is the verbal noun "crossing". The preposition is [[Lexicon/ܥܒܪ 2|ܥܒܪ]] *ʿabar* "across". The form follows the regular masculine template (*ʿabrâ*, like [[Lexicon/ܬܚܬܐ|ܬܚܬܐ]]). |

## Forms

Masculine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
