### Definitions

- **G** *baʿal* ܒܥܠ: to marry, to take as husband.
- **Gp** *bûʿal* ܒܘܥܠ: passive of G: to be married.
- **C** *šabʿal* ܫܒܥܠ: to give in marriage, to cause to marry.
- **Cp** *šûbʿal* ܫܘܒܥܠ: passive of C: to be given in marriage.
- **tD** *tibîʿâl* ܬܒܝܥܐܠ: to marry one another, to be wed.
- This verb has no D, Dp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܒܥܠ; Gp ܒܘܥܠ; C ܫܒܥܠ; Cp ܫܘܒܥܠ; tD ܬܒܝܥܐܠ.
