## Forms

The conjugation of a strong triliteral verb, following the paradigm on [[Morphophonology]] and the model page [[Lexicon/ܡܠܟ]]. The citation form is the 3m past. Each cell gives the vocalized Syriac, the romanization and the plain spelling.

### Pronunciation

The second radical is a guttural (ʿ). It is written in every form, but it is silent in the coda: *baʿlû* is pronounced as if *ba.lû*, and *yabʿal* as *yab.al*. A guttural cannot be geminated, and none of these forms needs it to be.

### Imperative

The imperative is the second-person non-past with its prefix removed. Every imperative is written with an exclamation mark. Where the stem starts with a consonant cluster, the first syllable carries a schwa (*ə*), and the stress falls on the next syllable. The masculine singular is spelled like the 3m past, and the mark distinguishes it.

| | singular | plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| m | *bəʿal!* ܒܥܠ! | *bəʿalnâ!* ܒܥܠܢܐ! |
| f | *bəʿalî!* ܒܥܠܝ! | *bəʿalnâ!* ܒܥܠܢܐ! |

### Passive

The passive changes the vowel after the first consonant to long *û*, written with a waw, so it shows in unvocalized text. Everything else follows the active paradigm. The participle and infinitive have no vowel after the first consonant to change (*ma-* and *la-* are prefixes), so they change the second vowel instead: *mabʿûl-* and *labʿûl*. The imperative has no passive.
