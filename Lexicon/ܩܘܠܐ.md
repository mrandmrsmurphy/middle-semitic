| Notes | The generic word for voice and sound, a person's or an animal's. Hebrew קוֹל "voice, sound, noise" (also thunder), Aramaic ܩܠܐ *qālā* "voice, sound, shout" and Arabic قَوْل "saying, word" are one word; Neo-Aramaic notes Ge'ez ቃል *ḳal* "word" as a cognate (Wiktionary). The diphthong contracts (*qawlâ* → *qôlâ*), as in [[Lexicon/ܚܘܠܐ\|ܚܘܠܐ]] *ḥôlâ*. The verb on the same root is [[Lexicon/ܩܘܠ\|ܩܘܠ]] "to sound forth". The plain "he said" is [[Lexicon/ܐܡܪ\|ܐܡܪ]]. |

## Forms

Masculine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
