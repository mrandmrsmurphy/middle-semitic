### Definitions

- **G** *ḥapar* ܚܦܪ: to dig.
- **Gp** *ḥûpar* ܚܘܦܪ: passive of G: to be dug.
- **D** *ḥîppar* ܚܝܦܪ: to dig thoroughly, to excavate.
- **Dp** *ḥûppar* ܚܘܦܪ: passive of D: to be excavated.
- **tD** *tiḥîppar* ܬܚܝܦܪ: to dig oneself in, to burrow.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܦܪ; Gp ܚܘܦܪ; D ܚܝܦܪ; Dp ܚܘܦܪ; tD ܬܚܝܦܪ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
