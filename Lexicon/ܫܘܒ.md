### Definitions

- **G** *šôb* ܫܘܒ: to return, to come back.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, C, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling, the past 3m of each stem is: G ܫܘܒ.

## Forms

A hollow verb: the central vowel is part of the root and is always present (here the contraction of *aw* to *ô*), so the stem stays CVC and the verb simply takes prefixes and suffixes. There is no marked stative. See [[Morphophonology]]. Each cell gives the romanization and the plain spelling.
