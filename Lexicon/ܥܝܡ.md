| Notes | An uninflecting particle marking the optional, emphatic progressive before the verb: with the non-past, ܗܠ ܫܡܣܐ ܥܝܡ ܬܙܪܚ *hal šamsâ ʿîm tazraḥ* "the sun is ongoingly shining"; with the past, *ʿîm zaraḥat* "was / has been ongoingly shining" — the particle attaches to either tense (the dialects instead build the past progressive with كَانَ عَمّ + the imperfect; MS generalizes). A clipping of the root ʿ-M-L via Arabic عمل (ʿamal "to do, work"); the model is the dialects' progressive marker عم (Levantine ʿam, itself a clipping of عَمَّال, per Wiktionary), spelled with î to stay distinct from the preposition [[Lexicon/ܥܡ\|ܥܡ]] ʿim "with". Hebrew and Aramaic have no progressive particle — the participle does the work (modern Hebrew זוֹרַחַת; Syriac participle + ܐܝܬ). See [[Morphosyntax#The progressive]]. |
