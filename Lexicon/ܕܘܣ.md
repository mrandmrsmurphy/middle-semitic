## Forms

A hollow verb: the central vowel is part of the root and is always present (a contraction or a pure mater, as the lexicon records), so the stem stays CVC and the verb simply takes prefixes and suffixes. There is no marked stative. The passive puts û after the first consonant of the form: it replaces the stem vowel in the past (*dôs* → *dûs*) and the prefix vowel in the non-past (*yadôs* → *yûdôs*). In unvocalized spelling the passive differs from the active only in the non-past (ܝܘܕܘܣ against ܝܕܘܣ), since ô and û are both written ܘ. See [[Morphophonology]].
