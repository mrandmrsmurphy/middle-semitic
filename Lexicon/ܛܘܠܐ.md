## Forms

Adjective from a II-waw root: *aw* contracts to *ô* throughout, as in [[Lexicon/ܛܘܒܐ\|ܛܘܒܐ]] *ṭôvâ*. It agrees with its noun in gender and number, and has the four forms below (masculine *-â*, feminine *-tâ*, plural *-în* and *-ût*). The construct forms are frozen and an archaism. A crypto-feminine noun takes the feminine forms. See [[Morphophonology]].
