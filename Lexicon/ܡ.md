## Forms

The clitic takes the pronominal suffixes (see [[Morphosyntax]]), with a single *n* where the full word doubles it. The inflected forms exist as words of their own:

### Fast speech

A clitic: it attaches to the following word and takes no stress. In fast speech it is acceptable to say it with a schwa for its vowel, as though *mi-* were *mə-*: *məMadrid* = from Madrid (for *miMadrid*). The schwa is marginal elsewhere; see [[Phonology]].
