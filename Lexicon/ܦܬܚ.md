### Definitions

- **G** *pataḥ* ܦܬܚ: to open, to begin.
- **Gp** *pûtaḥ* ܦܘܬܚ: passive of G: to be opened.
- **D** *pîttaḥ* ܦܝܬܚ: to open wide, to develop, to unfold.
- **Dp** *pûttaḥ* ܦܘܬܚ: passive of D: to be developed.
- **tD** *tipîttaḥ* ܬܦܝܬܚ: to develop, to blossom, to open out.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܦܬܚ; Gp ܦܘܬܚ; D ܦܝܬܚ; Dp ܦܘܬܚ; tD ܬܦܝܬܚ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
