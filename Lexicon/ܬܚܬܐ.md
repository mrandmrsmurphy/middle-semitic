| Notes | The place that is under: the underside, the beneath, the bottom of something. Hebrew תַּחַת is a masculine noun "the under part, bottom, underside" (Klein, BDB) as well as the preposition; Arabic تَحْت is the noun "the place that is below", declined like any noun, whose adverbial accusative تَحْتَ is the preposition. The preposition is [[Lexicon/ܬܚܬ\|ܬܚܬ]] *taḥat*. The form follows the regular masculine template (*taḥtâ*, like [[Lexicon/ܨܠܡܐ\|ܨܠܡܐ]]); the coda *ḥ* is written and not pronounced. |

## Forms

Masculine declension. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]]. The forms are built on the stem in the Latin row above.
