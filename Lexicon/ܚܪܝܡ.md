### Definitions

- **G** *ḥarîm* ܚܪܝܡ: to be forbidden, to be sacred.
- **D** *ḥîrram* ܚܝܪܡ: to forbid, to declare sacred or taboo.
- **Dp** *ḥûrram* ܚܘܪܡ: passive of D: to be forbidden, to be declared taboo.
- **C** *šaḥram* ܫܚܪܡ: to excommunicate, to confiscate, to boycott.
- **Cp** *šûḥram* ܫܘܚܪܡ: passive of C: to be excommunicated, to be boycotted.
- **tD** *tiḥîrram* ܬܚܝܪܡ: to become taboo, to be held sacred.
- This verb has no Gp stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܪܝܡ; D ܚܝܪܡ; Dp ܚܘܪܡ; C ܫܚܪܡ; Cp ܫܘܚܪܡ; tD ܬܚܝܪܡ.
