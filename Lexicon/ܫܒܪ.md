### Definitions

- **G** *šavar* ܫܒܪ: to break, to rupture.
- **Gp** *šûvar* ܫܘܒܪ: passive of G: to be broken.
- **D** *šîbbar* ܫܝܒܪ: to shatter, to smash to pieces.
- **Dp** *šûbbar* ܫܘܒܪ: passive of D: to be shattered.
- **tD** *tišîbbar* ܬܫܝܒܪ: to shatter, to break apart.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܫܒܪ; Gp ܫܘܒܪ; D ܫܝܒܪ; Dp ܫܘܒܪ; tD ܬܫܝܒܪ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
