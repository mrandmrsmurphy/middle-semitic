| Notes | To do, to make, to act: the general verb of making and doing (Exodus 20:4 *lâ tapʿal likâ ṣalmâ*, for Hebrew עָשָׂה). Hebrew פָּעַל *pāʿal* "do, make; act, operate", Aramaic פעל *pʿal* "work", Arabic فَعَلَ *faʿala* "do, act; fulfill" and Ugaritic *pʿl* (from memory) all go back to PS *\*paʕala* "to act" (Wiktionary). Branch vote: NW (Hebrew, Aramaic, Ugaritic) P-ʿ-L, Arabic P-ʿ-L; the only root that is "do, make" across Hebrew and Arabic, ruling by the root all branches share. The competing roots are not used for the general verb: Hebrew ʿ-Ś-Y (עָשָׂה; Hebrew and Ugaritic only), Aramaic ʿ-B-D ("do, make" in Aramaic only; Hebrew and Arabic have "work, serve", the sense of [[Lexicon/ܥܒܕܐ|ܥܒܕܐ]] "slave", kept for a future verb "to serve, to work"), Arabic Ṣ-N-ʿ (*ṣanaʿa*) and J-ʿ-L (*jaʿala*) (Arabic only) and ʿ-M-L ("toil" in NW, "do, work" in Arabic). Specific kinds of making keep their own verbs: [[Lexicon/ܒܢܝ|ܒܢܝ]] "build", [[Lexicon/ܒܪܐ|ܒܪܐ]] "create", [[Lexicon/ܨܘܪܬܐ|ܨܘܪܬܐ]] "form". Hebrew is rare in prose and common in poetry and Mishnaic Hebrew; Arabic uses it for "do". The D stem follows Arabic *faʿʿala* "activate". Consonants by the chart on [[Anthropology]]: all three radicals are straightforward. Hebrew, Arabic and Aramaic confirmed on Wiktionary; Ugaritic and the outgroups not checked |

### Definitions

- **G** *paʿal* ܦܥܠ: to do, to make, to act.
- **Gp** *pûʿal* ܦܘܥܠ: passive of G: to be done, to be made.
- **D** *pîʿâl* ܦܝܥܐܠ: to carry out, to put into action, to activate.
- **Dp** *pûʿâl* ܦܘܥܐܠ: passive of D: to be carried out.
- This verb has no C, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܦܥܠ; Gp ܦܘܥܠ; D ܦܝܥܐܠ; Dp ܦܘܥܐܠ.
