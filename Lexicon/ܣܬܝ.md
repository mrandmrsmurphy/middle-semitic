### Definitions

- **G** *satê* ܣܬܝ: to drink.
- **Gp** *sûtê* ܣܘܬܝ: passive of G: to be drunk (of a liquid).
- This verb has no D, Dp, C, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܣܬܝ; Gp ܣܘܬܝ.
