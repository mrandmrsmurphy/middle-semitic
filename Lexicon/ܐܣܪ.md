### Definitions

- **G** *âsar* ܐܣܪ: to bind, to imprison.
- **Gp** *ʾûsar* ܐܘܣܪ: passive of G: to be bound, to be imprisoned.
- **D** *ʾîssar* ܐܝܣܪ: to shackle, to bind fast.
- **Dp** *ʾûssar* ܐܘܣܪ: passive of D: to be shackled.
- **C** *šâsar* ܫܐܣܪ: to have (someone) imprisoned.
- **Cp** *šûʾsar* ܫܘܐܣܪ: passive of C: to be put in prison.
- **tD** *tiʾîssar* ܬܐܝܣܪ: to bind oneself, to be under obligation.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܐܣܪ; Gp ܐܘܣܪ; D ܐܝܣܪ; Dp ܐܘܣܪ; C ܫܐܣܪ; Cp ܫܘܐܣܪ; tD ܬܐܝܣܪ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
