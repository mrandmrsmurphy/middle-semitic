### Fast speech

A clitic: it attaches to the following word and takes no stress. In fast speech it is acceptable to say it with a schwa for its vowel, as though *ʿa-* were *ʿə-*: *ʿəMadrid* = on Madrid (for *ʿaMadrid*). The schwa is marginal elsewhere; see [[Phonology]].
