### Definitions

- **G** *walad* ܘܠܕ: to give birth, to bear.
- **Gp** *wûlad* ܘܘܠܕ: passive of G: to be born.
- **C** *šôlad* ܫܘܠܕ: to beget, to deliver (a child).
- **Cp** *šûwlad* ܫܘܘܠܕ: passive of C: to be begotten, to be delivered.
- This verb has no D, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܘܠܕ; Gp ܘܘܠܕ; C ܫܘܠܕ; Cp ܫܘܘܠܕ.
