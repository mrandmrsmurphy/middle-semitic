### Definitions

- **G** *napal* ܢܦܠ: to fall, to fall down.
- **Gp** *nûpal* ܢܘܦܠ: passive of G: to be fallen upon.
- **D** *nîppal* ܢܝܦܠ: to overthrow, to fell, to cast down.
- **Dp** *nûppal* ܢܘܦܠ: passive of D: to be overthrown.
- **C** *šanpal* ܫܢܦܠ: to drop, to let fall, to cause to fall.
- **Cp** *šûnpal* ܫܘܢܦܠ: passive of C: to be dropped.
- **tD** *tinîppal* ܬܢܝܦܠ: to fall upon, to attack, to prostrate oneself.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܢܦܠ; Gp ܢܘܦܠ; D ܢܝܦܠ; Dp ܢܘܦܠ; C ܫܢܦܠ; Cp ܫܘܢܦܠ; tD ܬܢܝܦܠ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
