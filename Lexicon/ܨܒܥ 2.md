### Definitions

- **G** *ṣabaʿ* ܨܒܥ: to point, to indicate.
- **Gp** *ṣûbaʿ* ܨܘܒܥ: passive of G: to be pointed out.
- **D** *ṣîbbaʿ* ܨܝܒܥ: to point out in detail, to designate one by one.
- **Dp** *ṣûbbaʿ* ܨܘܒܥ: passive of D: to be designated.
- This verb has no C, Cp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܨܒܥ; Gp ܨܘܒܥ; D ܨܝܒܥ; Dp ܨܘܒܥ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
