### Definitions

- **G** *ḥasîn* ܚܣܝܢ: to be good, to be handsome.
- **D** *ḥîssan* ܚܝܣܢ: to beautify, to improve.
- **Dp** *ḥûssan* ܚܘܣܢ: passive of D: to be beautified, to be improved.
- **C** *šaḥsan* ܫܚܣܢ: to do well by, to treat well.
- **Cp** *šûḥsan* ܫܘܚܣܢ: passive of C: to be treated well.
- **tD** *tiḥîssan* ܬܚܝܣܢ: to improve, to become better.
- This verb has no Gp stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܚܣܝܢ; D ܚܝܣܢ; Dp ܚܘܣܢ; C ܫܚܣܢ; Cp ܫܘܚܣܢ; tD ܬܚܝܣܢ.
