### Definitions

- **G** *malak* ܡܠܟ: to reign, to rule over (x).
- **Gp** *mûlak* ܡܘܠܟ: passive of G: to be ruled over.
- **C** *šamlak* ܫܡܠܟ: to enthrone.
- **Cp** *šûmlak* ܫܘܡܠܟ: passive of C: to be enthroned.
- This verb has no D, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܡܠܟ; Gp ܡܘܠܟ; C ܫܡܠܟ; Cp ܫܘܡܠܟ.
