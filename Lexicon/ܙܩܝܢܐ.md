| Notes | The adjective applies to people of any gender, with ordinary agreement. The qatîl pattern adapts Hebrew זָקֵן. The related Middle Semitic noun [[Lexicon/ܙܩܢܐ\|ܙܩܢܐ]] means “beard”; the Arabic, Aramaic, Ugaritic and Akkadian forms belong to the beard or chin sense. The [[Anthropology]] chart maps *ḏ* to ܙ and *ḳ* to ܩ. Branch vote: NW (Hebrew) Z-Q-N “elderly,” Aramaic S-B “elder”; Arabic uses other roots for ordinary “elderly”; outgroup Akkadian preserves “beard”; ruling by the Hebrew age sense on the established Middle Semitic root. Compare [[Lexicon/ܥܬܝܩܐ\|ܥܬܝܩܐ]] “old” of things.
