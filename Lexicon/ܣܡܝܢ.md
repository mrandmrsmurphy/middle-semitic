### Definitions

- **G** *samîn* ܣܡܝܢ: to be fat.
- **D** *sîmman* ܣܝܡܢ: to fatten, to make plump.
- **Dp** *sûmman* ܣܘܡܢ: passive of D: to be fattened.
- **C** *šasman* ܫܣܡܢ: to fatten up, to enrich.
- **Cp** *šûsman* ܫܘܣܡܢ: passive of C: to be fattened up.
- **tD** *tisîmman* ܬܣܝܡܢ: to put on weight, to grow fat.
- This verb has no Gp stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܣܡܝܢ; D ܣܝܡܢ; Dp ܣܘܡܢ; C ܫܣܡܢ; Cp ܫܘܣܡܢ; tD ܬܣܝܡܢ.
