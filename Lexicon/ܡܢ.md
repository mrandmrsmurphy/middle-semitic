## Forms

One of the eight small prepositions that take the pronominal suffixes (see [[Morphosyntax]]). The inflected forms exist as words of their own:

| | form | gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1cs | [[Lexicon/ܡܢܢܝ\|ܡܢܢܝ]] *minnî* | from me |
| 2ms | [[Lexicon/ܡܢܟܐ\|ܡܢܟܐ]] *minkâ* | from you (m) |
| 2fs | [[Lexicon/ܡܢܟܝ\|ܡܢܟܝ]] *minkî* | from you (f) |
| 3ms | [[Lexicon/ܡܢܗܘ\|ܡܢܗܘ]] *minhû* | from him |
| 3fs | [[Lexicon/ܡܢܗܐ\|ܡܢܗܐ]] *minhâ* | from her |
| 1cp | [[Lexicon/ܡܢܢܘ\|ܡܢܢܘ]] *minnû* | from us |
| 2cp | [[Lexicon/ܡܢܟܘܡ\|ܡܢܟܘܡ]] *minkûm* | from y'all |
| 3cp | [[Lexicon/ܡܢܗܘܡ\|ܡܢܗܘܡ]] *minhûm* | from them |

The full word keeps its stress. Before a following word it has a clitic form, [[Lexicon/ܡ|ܡ]] *mi-*, with its own suffixed forms (*minî*, *mikâ*, ...); in fast speech that clitic may be said *mə-*.
