### Definitions

- **G** *âkal* ܐܟܠ: to eat.
- **Gp** *ʾûkal* ܐܘܟܠ: passive of G: to be eaten.
- **D** *ʾîkkal* ܐܝܟܠ: to devour, to wolf.
- **Dp** *ʾûkkal* ܐܘܟܠ: passive of D: to be devoured.
- **C** *šâkal* ܫܐܟܠ: to feed.
- **Cp** *šûʾkal* ܫܘܐܟܠ: passive of C: to be fed.
- **tD** *tiʾîkkal* ܬܐܝܟܠ: to be eaten away, to become corroded.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܐܟܠ; Gp ܐܘܟܠ; D ܐܝܟܠ; Dp ܐܘܟܠ; C ܫܐܟܠ; Cp ܫܘܐܟܠ; tD ܬܐܝܟܠ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
