### Passive

The passive changes the vowel after the first consonant to long *û*, written with a waw. In the passive non-past the root-initial mater reverts to a consonant after the *û* (aleph as a glottal stop), which keeps the passive distinct from the active. The participle and infinitive change the second vowel. The imperative has no passive.
