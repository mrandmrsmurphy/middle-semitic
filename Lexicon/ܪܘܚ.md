### Definitions

- **G** *rôḥ* ܪܘܚ: to spread out, to be spacious.
- **D** *rîwwaḥ* ܪܝܘܚ: to refresh, to air out.
- **Dp** *rûwwaḥ* ܪܘܘܚ: passive of D: to be refreshed.
- **C** *šarwaḥ* ܫܪܘܚ: to make room, to give relief, to profit.
- **Cp** *šûrwaḥ* ܫܘܪܘܚ: passive of C: to be relieved.
- **tD** *tirîwwaḥ* ܬܪܝܘܚ: to take the air, to be refreshed.
- This verb has no Gp stem.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܪܘܚ; D ܪܝܘܚ; Dp ܪܘܘܚ; C ܫܪܘܚ; Cp ܫܘܪܘܚ; tD ܬܪܝܘܚ.
