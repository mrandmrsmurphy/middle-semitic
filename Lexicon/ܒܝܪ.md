## Forms

The conjugation of a geminate verb (root ܒ-ܪ-ܪ, the only one), following the paradigm on [[Morphophonology]] and the model page [[Lexicon/ܡܠܟ]]. The doubled consonant is written once in the plain spelling. In the past it is simplified before an ending that starts with a consonant and returns before one that starts with a vowel (*bîrtâ*, *bîrrat*); the non-past and the other forms follow the strong template. The citation form is the 3m past. Each cell gives the vocalized Syriac, the romanization and the plain spelling.

### Past

Stative verb: the vowel after the first consonant is *î* in the finite forms, and there is no passive.

### Imperative

The imperative is the second-person non-past with its prefix removed. Every imperative is written with an exclamation mark. Where the stem starts with a consonant cluster, the first syllable carries a schwa (*ə*), and the stress falls on the next syllable.
