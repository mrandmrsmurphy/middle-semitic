| Notes | Hebrew צִפּוֹר and Aramaic ܨܶܦܪܳܐ are the general word for "bird"; Arabic عُصْفُور is usually "sparrow", and Ugaritic *ṣpr* is "bird" (also a verb "to whistle"). Proto-Semitic *ṣapir-* is "small bird"; a second Proto-Semitic word, *ʕVṣṣūr-* "bird", survives only in Akkadian and Ugaritic *ʿṣr*. The spelling has ܘ for the long û, which keeps it apart from Syriac ܨܦܪܐ "morning". |

## Forms
