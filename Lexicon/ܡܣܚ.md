### Definitions

- **G** *masaḥ* ܡܣܚ: to anoint, to rub with oil.
- **Gp** *mûsaḥ* ܡܘܣܚ: passive of G: to be anointed.
- **D** *mîssaḥ* ܡܝܣܚ: to wipe off, to rub down, to anoint solemnly.
- **Dp** *mûssaḥ* ܡܘܣܚ: passive of D: to be wiped off, to be anointed solemnly.
- **tD** *timîssaḥ* ܬܡܝܣܚ: to rub oneself, to anoint oneself.
- This verb has no C, Cp stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܡܣܚ; Gp ܡܘܣܚ; D ܡܝܣܚ; Dp ܡܘܣܚ; tD ܬܡܝܣܚ. Gp and Dp are spelled alike unless the shadda is written, as in Hebrew and Arabic.
