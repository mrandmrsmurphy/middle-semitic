| Notes | The region where the upper leg meets the pelvis. Initial ܘ follows reconstructed *warik-* and Arabic; Hebrew and Jewish Babylonian Aramaic have initial y. Branch vote: NW (Hebrew and Jewish Babylonian Aramaic) Y-R-K “thigh, hip,” with Ugaritic “flank, side”; Arabic W-R-K “hip, haunch”; ruling by the shared root, with the Arabic sense used for “hip” and [[Lexicon/ܦܚܙܐ\|ܦܚܙܐ]] for “thigh.” [Comparative evidence](https://joverfelt.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/the-semitic-languages-an-international-handbook-handbucc88cher-zur-sprach-und-kommunikationswissenschaft-by-stefan-weninger-geoffrey-khan-michael-p.-streck-janet-c.-e.-watson-.pdf). |
