## Forms

One of the eight small prepositions that take the pronominal suffixes (see [[Middle Semitic/Morphosyntax]]). The inflected forms exist as words of their own:

| --- | --- | --- |
| 1cs | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܠܢܝ\|ܠܢܝ]] *linî* | for me |
| 2ms | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܠܟܐ\|ܠܟܐ]] *likâ* | for you (m) |
| 2fs | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܠܟܝ\|ܠܟܝ]] *likî* | for you (f) |
| 3ms | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܠܗܘ\|ܠܗܘ]] *lihû* | for him |
| 3fs | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܠܗܐ\|ܠܗܐ]] *lihâ* | for her |
| 1cp | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܠܢܘ\|ܠܢܘ]] *linû* | for us |
| 2cp | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܠܟܘܡ\|ܠܟܘܡ]] *likûm* | for y'all |
| 3cp | [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܠܗܘܡ\|ܠܗܘܡ]] *lihûm* | for them |

### Fast speech

A clitic: it attaches to the following word and takes no stress. In fast speech it is acceptable to say it with a schwa for its vowel, as though *li-* were *lə-*: *ləMadrid* = to Madrid (for *liMadrid*). The schwa is marginal elsewhere; see [[Middle Semitic/Phonology]].
