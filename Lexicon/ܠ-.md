## Forms

One of the eight small prepositions that take the pronominal suffixes (see [[Morphosyntax]]). The inflected forms exist as words of their own:

| | form | gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1cs | [[Lexicon/ܠܢܝ\|ܠܢܝ]] *linî* | for me |
| 2ms | [[Lexicon/ܠܟܐ\|ܠܟܐ]] *likâ* | for you (m) |
| 2fs | [[Lexicon/ܠܟܝ\|ܠܟܝ]] *likî* | for you (f) |
| 3ms | [[Lexicon/ܠܗܘ\|ܠܗܘ]] *lihû* | for him |
| 3fs | [[Lexicon/ܠܗܐ\|ܠܗܐ]] *lihâ* | for her |

| 2cp | [[Lexicon/ܠܟܘܡ\|ܠܟܘܡ]] *likûm* | for y'all |
| 3cp | [[Lexicon/ܠܗܘܡ\|ܠܗܘܡ]] *lihûm* | for them |

### Fast speech

A clitic: it attaches to the following word and takes no stress. In fast speech it is acceptable to say it with a schwa for its vowel, as though *li-* were *lə-*: *ləMadrid* = to Madrid (for *liMadrid*). The schwa is marginal elsewhere; see [[Phonology]].
