## Forms

A hollow verb: the central vowel is part of the root and is always present (a contraction or a pure mater, as the lexicon records), so the stem stays CVC and the verb simply takes prefixes and suffixes. There is no marked stative and no passive: the active and passive cannot be told apart by form. See [[Middle Semitic/Morphophonology]]. Each cell gives the romanization and the plain spelling.

### Imperative

The imperative is the second-person non-past without its prefix. Every imperative is written with an exclamation mark. Where the stem starts with a consonant cluster, the first syllable carries a schwa (*ə*), and the stress falls on the next syllable.
