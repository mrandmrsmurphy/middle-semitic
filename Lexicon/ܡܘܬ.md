### Definitions

- **G** *môt* ܡܘܬ: to die.
- **C** *šamwat* ܫܡܘܬ: to put to death, to kill.
- **Cp** *šûmwat* ܫܘܡܘܬ: passive of C: to be put to death.
- This verb has no Gp, D, Dp, tD stems.

### Recognizing the stems

In the unvocalized spelling (the gemination mark is optional and left out), the past 3m of each stem is: G ܡܘܬ; C ܫܡܘܬ; Cp ܫܘܡܘܬ.
