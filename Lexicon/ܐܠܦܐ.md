## Forms

Masculine declension. Chiefly a noun: 'a thousand' is the noun *alpâ*, counted and modified like any other noun. There is no dual: the dual is frozen and only body parts, glasses and pants have one. The construct state is frozen and an archaism. See [[Morphophonology]].
