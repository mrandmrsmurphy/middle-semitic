## Forms

The conjugation of a strong triliteral verb, following the paradigm on [[Morphophonology]] and the model page [[Lexicon/ܡܠܟ]]. The citation form is the 3m past. Each cell gives the vocalized Syriac, the romanization and the plain spelling.

### Past

Stative verb: the vowel after the first consonant is *î* in the finite forms, and there is no passive.

### Imperative

The imperative is the second-person non-past with its prefix removed. Every imperative is written with an exclamation mark. Where the stem starts with a consonant cluster, the first syllable carries a schwa (*ə*), and the stress falls on the next syllable. The masculine singular is spelled like the 3m past, and the mark distinguishes it.

| | singular | plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| m | *səlam!* ܣܠܡ! | *səlamnâ!* ܣܠܡܢܐ! |
| f | *səlamî!* ܣܠܡܝ! | *səlamnâ!* ܣܠܡܢܐ! |
