# Sophomore List

> Parent: [[Lexipedia/Lexipedia|Lexipedia]]

Mark Rosenfelder, in his *Language Construction Kit*, has a list that comes after Swadesh, which he calls his "Sophomore List".

Each of the 15 Universal Languages should develop its own version of this list.
