# Middle Semitic Vault Instructions

This file and `AGENTS.md` are duplicates — always keep them byte-identical; edit both (or edit one and copy it over the other) whenever instructions change.

This vault is an Obsidian vault documenting **Middle Semitic** (ܫܡܥ ܡܪܟܙ šamâ markaz), the Semitic Universal Language: a blend of Hebrew and Arabic written in Syriac, zoned for the Middle East and North Africa. It spun out of the UL vault (`/Users/robertmarshallmurphy/Documents/UL`) on 2026-10-28; UL keeps a hub page and the cross-UL comparison pages.

## Memory system (read first)

- Read `AIOS/memory-index.md` first, every session, before touching anything. It points to every durable memory (vault layout, lexicon conventions, grammar rulings). Do not skip it and re-derive things from scratch.
- Everything meant to survive across sessions goes in `AIOS/memory/`, indexed in `AIOS/memory-index.md`. Session-by-session history of what was created/changed/decided goes in `logs/log.md` (append-only).
- The vault is a git repo; commit after scripted bulk changes.

## Content pages vs. log — the rule that matters most

- Content pages document **current state only**. No date stamps, no "decided by the user on X," no correction narratives. If a decision changes, update the page's fact in place, written as if it had been right from the start.
- All history — what was decided when, what got corrected, who caught what — belongs exclusively in `logs/log.md` (append-only, never edit past entries).
- Citation-confidence / evidentiary-weight flags ("plausible extension, not directly attested") are facts about the claim itself and are fine on content pages — terse and undated.
- `AIOS/` is exempt from this rule — tracking what changed and when is its job.

## Source record
