# ܠ-ܚ-ܨ

> Parent: [[Roots/Roots.base|Roots]]

Triliteral root. **Reserved — no words coined yet.**

Near-translation of [[Lexicon/ܥܨܪ|ܥܨܪ]] "to squeeze" (Swadesh № 130): "pressure, to oppress." Hebrew לָחַץ "squeeze, press; oppress", with the noun לַחַץ "oppression, distress" and "pressure" in Modern Hebrew (Klein, BDB). BDB's cognates: Arabic لَحَّصَ, Christian Palestinian Aramaic ܠܚܨ, Mandaic, and Neo-Syriac ܚܵܠܸܨ (with metathesis). Arabic does not use it for squeeze, so MS does not take it for the Swadesh item.
