# ܣ-ܩ-ܝ

> Parent: [[Roots/Roots.base|Roots]]

Triliteral root. **Reserved — to water, give drink, irrigate.**

Proto-Semitic \*šqy. Hebrew הִשְׁקָה and Arabic سَقَى share the watering and giving-to-drink senses. Akkadian *šaqû* also means “give to drink, irrigate.”
