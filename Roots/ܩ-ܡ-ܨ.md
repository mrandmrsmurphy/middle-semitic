# ܩ-ܡ-ܨ

> Parent: [[Roots/Roots.base|Roots]]

Triliteral root. **Reserved — no words coined yet.**

Near-translation of [[Lexicon/ܥܨܪ|ܥܨܪ]] "to squeeze" (Swadesh № 130): "to clench, to take a handful." Hebrew קָמַץ "enclose with the hand, grasp, take a handful, close" (Lev 2:2, 5:12, Num 5:26) and in Mishnaic Hebrew "press together"; the noun קֹמֶץ "a handful" (Klein). Probably separate from Q-M-Ṭ (Syriac ܩܡܛ, Hebrew קָמַט "compress, fold"), which is not reserved. Arabic and Aramaic cognates not checked.
