# ܥ-ܒ-ܪ

Triliteral root. Words built on it:

- [[Lexicon/ܥܒܪ 2|ܥܒܪ]] (ʿabar) — across, beyond, through
- [[Lexicon/ܥܒܪܐ|ܥܒܪܐ]] (ʿabrâ) — the other side, the far bank, the region beyond
