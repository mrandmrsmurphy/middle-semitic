# ܨ-ܪ-ܪ

> Parent: [[Roots/Roots.base|Roots]]

Triliteral root. Words built on it:

- [[Lexicon/ܨܪܬܐ|ܨܪܬܐ]] (ṣartâ) — co-wife (negative)
- [[Lexicon/ܨܪܪܐ|ܨܪܪܐ]] (ṣarrâ) — narrow

Near-translation of [[Lexicon/ܥܨܪ|ܥܨܪ]] "to squeeze" (Swadesh № 130): "to constrict, to tie tight." Hebrew צָרַר "bind, tie up; be narrow, cramped", Arabic صَرَّ "bind tight", Aramaic צְרַר, Syriac ܨܰܪ (BDB). A verb on this root is not yet coined. Caution: the MS root also holds the ṣ́-r-r words (Hebrew צָרַר "show hostility", Arabic ضَرَّ "harm", the co-wife ܨܪܬܐ), because the chart merges ṣ́ with ṣ.
