# ܐ-ܪ-ܟ

> Parent: [[Roots/Roots]]

Triliteral root. **No words are coined on it.**

The ancient word for 'long': Proto-Semitic \*ʾarik-, Hebrew אָרֹךְ, Aramaic (Syriac) ܐܰܪܝܟܳܐ. In Arabic, however, the root is used only of a long *time*, not of physical length. MS therefore passes it over and coins 'long' from Ṭ-W-L instead: [[Lexicon/ܛܘܠܐ|ܛܘܠܐ]] *ṭôlâ* (Swadesh № 28).
