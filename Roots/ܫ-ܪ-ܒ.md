# ܫ-ܪ-ܒ

> Parent: [[Roots/Roots.base|Roots]]

Triliteral root. **Reserved — to sip, suck, absorb.**

Proto-Semitic \*śrb in Kogan’s reconstruction. Arabic شَرِبَ “drink” and Geʿez *śäräbä* are evidence for the family. Rabbinic Hebrew שָׂרַף “sip, suck, absorb” is a possible cognate with a different final consonant.
