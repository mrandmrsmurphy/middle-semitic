# ܚ-ܢ-ܩ

> Parent: [[Roots/Roots.base|Roots]]

Triliteral root. **Reserved — no words coined yet.**

Near-translation of [[Lexicon/ܥܨܪ|ܥܨܪ]] "to squeeze" (Swadesh № 130): "to choke, to strangle." Hebrew חָנַק "strangle, choke" and in Mishnaic Hebrew "press hard"; the noun חֶנֶק "strangulation" (Klein). Arabic خَنَقَ "choke" and Syriac ܚܢܩ are from memory, unchecked. Chart: PS \*ḫ is MS ḥ ܚ, as in Hebrew ח.
