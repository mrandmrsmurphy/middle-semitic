# ܪ-ܘ-ܝ

> Parent: [[Roots/Roots.base|Roots]]

Triliteral root. **Reserved — to be slaked, be saturated, have one’s fill of liquid.**

Hebrew רָוָה “drink one’s fill, be saturated” and Arabic رَوِيَ “have one’s thirst quenched” share the saturation sense. Syriac and Geʿez also preserve the family.
