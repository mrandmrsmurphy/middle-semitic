# ܨ-ܘ-ܩ

> Parent: [[Roots/Roots.base|Roots]]

Triliteral root. **Reserved — no words coined yet.**

Near-translation of [[Lexicon/ܥܨܪ|ܥܨܪ]] "to squeeze" (Swadesh № 130): "to press upon, to constrain, to distress." Hebrew הֵצִיק "constrain, press upon, distress" and the noun צוֹק "constraint" (Dan 9:25; Klein). Arabic ضَاقَ "be narrow" and Syriac ܥܝܩ (the narrow cell of Swadesh № 34) are from memory, unchecked. Chart: Hebrew צ, Arabic ض and Aramaic ע all go back to PS \*ṣ́, which is MS ṣ ܨ.
