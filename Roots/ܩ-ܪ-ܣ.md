# ܩ-ܪ-ܣ

> Parent: [[Roots/Roots.base|Roots]]

Triliteral root. **Reserved — no words coined yet.**

Reserved for "to freeze, to be icy", a near-translation of [[Lexicon/ܩܪܝܪ|ܩܪܝܪ]] "to be cold" (Swadesh № 181). Syriac ܩܪܫ "to be frozen", Phoenician *qrš* "to become frozen" and Arabic قَرِسَ "to be bitterly cold; to freeze (of water)" share the root (Kogan's wordlist, PS \*ḳrš). Chart: PS \*š is MS s ܣ (Hebrew/Aramaic š, Arabic س), so the root is spelled with ܣ, not ܫ, as in [[Lexicon/ܣܚܝܢܐ|ܣܚܝܢܐ]]. Hebrew has no verb on this root.
