# ܩ-ܛ-ܢ

> Parent: [[Middle Semitic/Roots/Roots]]

Triliteral root. **Reserved — no words coined yet.**

Hebrew קָטָן 'small' is very Northwest Semitic; MS saves this root for 'slender'. 'Small' (also 'young', 'minor') is [[Middle Semitic/Lexicon/ܨܥܝܪܐ|ܨܥܝܪܐ]] *ṣaʿîrâ* (Swadesh № 32), on the root [[Middle Semitic/Roots/ܨ-ܥ-ܪ|ܨ-ܥ-ܪ]].
